Yoga (darśana) is the school systematized by Patañjali in the Yoga Sūtras (c. 2nd c. BCE–2nd c. CE), which adopts Sāṃkhya metaphysics and prescribes the eight-limbed path (ashtanga: yama, niyama, āsana, prāṇāyāma, pratyāhāra, dhāraṇā, dhyāna, samādhi) to still the modifications of the mind (citta-vṛtti-nirodha). Liberation (kaivalya) is the cessation of the guṇa-afflictions and the realization of the pure Self (puruṣa) distinct from prakṛti. The term 'yoga' also denotes the practical discipline of the Gītā (karma-, jñāna-, bhakti-yoga) and contemporary physical and meditation traditions.
