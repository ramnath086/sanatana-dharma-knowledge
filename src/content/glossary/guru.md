Guru (गुरु) is the spiritual teacher/preceptor (the dispeller of darkness), the second of the three jewels (triratna) and the conduit of the paramparā (lineage) of knowledge. The guru is honored as the 'second birth' (dwitīya-janma) and the 'eye of knowledge' (jñāna-cakṣu); the Gītā (4.38) declares jñāna greater than karma because the guru reveals the self (ātman). The guru-disciple (guru-śiṣya) relationship structures the transmission of the Vedas (gurukula), the Vedānta, and the Tantric and devotional (bhakti) lineages; the teacher is the living embodiment of the tradition (paramparā-vāṇī).
