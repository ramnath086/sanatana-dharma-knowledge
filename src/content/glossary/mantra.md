Mantra (मन्त्र) is a sacred utterance (the 'mind-tool' = man + tra) used in invocation, protection, and transformation, the formula of the Vedic (Ṛg) and the tantric (tantric) disciplines. The Gītā (6.16–17) and the Mantra-ratnākara define its efficacy as the syllable (bīja), the name (nāma), and the formula (vyāpti), with the breath (prāṇa) and concentration (dhāraṇā). Categories include the pauruṣa (personal) and the a-puruṣa (impersonal), the dhyāna-mantra (e.g. Gāyatrī), and the kāla-mantra (e.g. Asya-vacana). A mantra's power (śakti) is believed to be activated by the guru's empowerment (dīkṣā).
