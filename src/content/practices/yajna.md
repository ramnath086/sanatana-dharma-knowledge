A ritual category with different textual, historical and contemporary contexts. The word 'yajña' (sacrifice) can denote (1) the Vedic fire-ritual (e.g. the agṇi-hotra, the soma-yajña, the ṣrauta yajñas), (2) the offering itself (the 'devayana' / 'pindārtha' offerings to the gods, ancestors, and guests), and (3) the broader sense of 'righteous action performed as sacrifice' (karma-yajña) — as elaborated in the Bhagavad Gītā. Specific procedures and vidhānas are given under the individual ritual records referenced by agama, śrauta, and smṛti sources.
