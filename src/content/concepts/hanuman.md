Hanumān (हनुमान्) is the vānara (forest-being) and devoted servant of Rāma in the Vālmiki Rāmāyaṇa and the Sundara Kāṇḍa, the son of the wind-god. He leaps to Lanka, finds Sītā, and burns the city; he is the embodiment of service (sevā) and devotion (bhakti). Worshipped on Hanumān Jayantī in the month of Kārtika, he is the remover of obstacles (vighna-hara) and protector of thresholds. His iconography (mace, mountain, simian form) is set in dhyāna-slokas and his name ends many a sādhaka's practice.
