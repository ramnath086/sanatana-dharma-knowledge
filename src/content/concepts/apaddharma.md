The Dharmaśāstra concept of āpaddharma (dharma in times of distress/emergency): the relaxation of normal rules for survival, the hierarchy of permissible actions, and the principle that normal dharma resumes when the emergency passes; its textual basis in Manusmṛti and other Smṛtis is represented here as a catalogue entry.
