Kalpa is the third Vedāṅga, the manual of ritual procedure (śrauta and gṛhya rites) prescribing the correct form, sequence, and utensils of sacrificial and domestic ceremonies, derived from the Brāhmaṇas. The Sūtra form (Shrauta-sūtras of Apastamba, Baudhayana, Āpastamba; Gṛhya-sūtras) renders the Brāhmaṇa prescriptions operational. The six Kalpa-sūtras (Āpastamba, Baudhāyana, etc.) were the most quoted and transmitted Vedāṅga and supplied the liturgical grammar for the later Dharmaśāstra and manual literature. Kalpa defines dharma as practice (acara) embedded in ritual performance.
