The Sāṃkhya enumeration of twenty-five tattvas (principles): prakṛti (primordial matter), mahat (buddhi), ahaṃkāra, five tanmātras, five mahābhūtas, five jñānendriyas, five karmendriyas, manas, and puruṣa (consciousness); their evolutionary sequence and interrelations are represented here as a catalogue entry.
