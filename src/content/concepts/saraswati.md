Sarasvatī (सरस्वती) is the Vedic goddess of speech (Vāc), learning (vidyā), music (saṃgīta), and the arts, the patron of students. The Sarasvatī-vandanā opens the study of the Vedas and the performing arts in the sandhyā ritual. The Sarasvatī-purāṇa recounts her birth from the ocean-churning. She is depicted as white-robed (śukla), holding the vīṇā, the book (pustaka), and the swan (hamsa) — the symbol of discriminative wisdom (viveka). She is honoured especially by students and artists.
