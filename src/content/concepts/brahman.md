Brahman (ब्रह्मन्) is the supreme reality or ultimate ground of being in the Vedānta tradition, described through two complementary registers: neti neti (not this, not this) negation in the Bāṣkala Upaniṣad and affirmative definition (sat-caitā-tāṇḍya-vijñāna) in the Taittirīya. Brahman is the material, efficient, and final cause in different schools: the impersonal Brahman of Śaṅkara, the personal Brahman of Rāmānuja and Madhva, and the bhakti-oriented Brahman of the Gauḍīya tradition. It is the substratum of cognition (sat), consciousness (caitanya), and bliss (ānanda), the source and goal of all beings.
