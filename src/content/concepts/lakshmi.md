Lakṣmī (लक्ष्मी) is the goddess of prosperity and auspiciousness, the principal consort (śakti) of Viṣṇu/Nārāyaṇa. Born in the churning of the ocean (samudra-manthan) as the daughter of the Ocean, she is the four-fold Lakṣmī: Sīdhārthī, Gajalakṣmī, Mahālakṣmī, and Padma-lakṣmī. She is the focus of Diwali worship (Lakṣmī-pūjā) and appears in the Sākambhalīṇi and the Padma-Purāṇa. She is depicted holding lotus, conch, discus, and book.
