The Navagraha (nine celestial influencers): Sūrya (Sun), Candra (Moon), Maṅgala (Mars), Budha (Mercury), Bṛhaspati (Jupiter), Śukra (Venus), Śani (Saturn), Rāhu (ascending node), Ketu (descending node); their role in Jyotiṣa (Vedic astrology), their temples (Navagraha sthalas), and their ritual propitiation (graha-śānti) are represented here as a catalogue entry.
