Kāma (काम) is desire and the erotic, the third of the four Puruṣārthas, the vital force that animates embodied life. The Kāma-sūtra (Vātsyāyana) and the Kāma-saṅgraha systematize the art of love, the stages of courtship, and the social arts. In the Gītā (2.45) kāma is part of the worldly three (triguṇa-viṣaya) that bind the self; yet in its right place (dharma-saṃyukta) it is a legitimate aim and the companion of artha and mokṣa. The Kāma-sūtra's seven parts are the classic schema of the arts of love.
