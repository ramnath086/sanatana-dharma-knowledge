The Maitrāyaṇīya Śākhā is a recension of the Kṛṣṇa Yajurveda; its texts include the Maitrāyaṇīya Saṃhitā, Brāhmaṇa, Āraṇyaka, and Upaniṣad; represented here as a catalogue entry.
