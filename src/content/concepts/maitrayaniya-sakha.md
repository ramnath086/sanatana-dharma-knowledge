The Maitrāyaṇīya Śākha is a recension of the White (Śukla) Yajur Veda, the source of the Maitrāyaṇīya Saṃhitā, Brāhmaṇa, Āraṇyaka, and Upaniṣad. The Śākha preserves the 'three cities' (triloka) doctrine of the Self and is notable for the 'mind' (manas) and the 'breath' (prāṇa) passages that influenced the Buddhist and Jain psychology of the mind. The Maitrāyaṇīya Upaniṣad is one of the 'minor' (upaniṣad) sources for the doctrine of the inner (antarātman) versus the outer (bāhira) self.
