Padma-tīrtha (the 'lotus-tīrtha') is a sacred pilgrimage site associated with the lotus (padma) of Viṣṇu, most notably the sacred pool at Vārāṇasī (Kāśī) where the Ganga is said to descend and the lotus-navel of Nārāyaṇa (Viṣṇu) is bathed. The padma-tirtha is the 'lotus-place' (padma-sthala) for the rites of the dead (antyeṣṭi) and for the removal of sins (pāpa) and the birth in Kāśī. It is distinct from the Padmanābha temple of Travancore.
