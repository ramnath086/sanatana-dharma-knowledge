The classification of vrata (vows/observances) in Smṛti and Purāṇic tradition: nitya (daily), naimittika (occasion-specific), kāmya (desire-motivated), the upavāsa (fasting) types, the kāla-vratas (time-based), and the kāyika-vācika-mānasa distinction; their role in purification and merit are represented here as a catalogue entry.
