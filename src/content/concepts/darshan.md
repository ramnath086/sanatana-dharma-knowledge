Darśana (auspicious sight): the act of beholding a deity, guru, or sacred object in Hindu practice; its theological significance as a two-way exchange of grace (the deity sees the devotee, the devotee sees the deity); its role in temple worship, pilgrimage (tīrtha-yātrā), and guru-seva are represented here as a catalogue entry.
