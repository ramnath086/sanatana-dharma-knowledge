The sixteen principal saṃskāras (sacraments) in Dharmaśāstra: garbhādhāna (conception), puṃsavana (ensuring male offspring), sīmantonnayana (parting hair), jātakarma (birth), nāmakaraṇa (naming), niṣkramaṇa (first outing), annaprāśana (first solid food), cūḍākaraṇa (tonsure), upanayana (sacred thread), vedārambha (Vedic study), keśānta (first shaving), samāvartana (completion of studies), vivāha (marriage), and antyeṣṭi (funeral); their timing, mantras, and social significance are represented here as a catalogue entry.
