The sixteen upacāras (offerings) in formal Pūjā: āvāhana (invocation), āsana (seat), pādyā (water for feet), arghya (water for hands), ācamana (sipping water), snāna (bath), vastra (clothing), yajñopavīta (sacred thread), gandha (sandalwood), puṣpa (flowers), dhūpa (incense), dīpa (lamp), naivedya (food), tāmbūla (betel), nīrājana (waving lights), and mantra-puṣpāñjali; their sequence and significance are represented here as a catalogue entry.
