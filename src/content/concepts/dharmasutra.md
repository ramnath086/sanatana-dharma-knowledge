The Dharma-sūtra (the 'thread/sutra of dharma') is the earliest genre of Dharma literature, composed in the terse sutra style and attached to each Vedic school (Apastamba, Gautama, and Vāśiṣṭha of the Yajur; the Āpastamba of the Ṛg). It treats the law (dharma) — the duties of varṇas and āśramas, the life-cycle rites (saṃskāra), and the 'means' (upāya) — in the 'three worlds' (triloka). It is the direct antecedent of the metrical Smṛtis (Manu) and the fountain of the Dharmaśāstra tradition.
