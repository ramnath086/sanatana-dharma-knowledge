The Mīmāṃsā theory of sphoṭa (the eternal, unchanging word-essence) as the bearer of meaning, distinct from the audible sound (dhvani); the debate between Kumārila Bhaṭṭa (who rejects sphoṭa) and the Grammarians (who affirm it); the epistemological status of Vedic words and their relation to meaning are represented here as a catalogue entry.
