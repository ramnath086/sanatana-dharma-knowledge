Sītā (सीता), born of the earth (Bhūmi), is the wife of Rāma and the archetype of conjugal fidelity (pativrata) in the Vālmiki Rāmāyaṇa. Raised by Janaka, she accompanies Rāma into exile and is abducted by Ravana. The fire-test (āgni-prarohaṇa) and the later forest exile and agni-cleansing test the bounds of trust. Her birth-story and her final return to the earth close the epic's treatment of dharma and the feminine principle (stri-dharma).
