The Taittirīya Śākhā is the principal surviving recension of the Kṛṣṇa (Black) Yajurveda, prevalent in South India; its texts include the Taittirīya Saṃhitā, Brāhmaṇa, Āraṇyaka, and Upaniṣad; represented here as a catalogue entry.
