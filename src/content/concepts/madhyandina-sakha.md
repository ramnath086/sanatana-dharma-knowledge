The Mādhyandina Śākhā is a principal recension of the Śukla (White) Yajurveda, prevalent in North India; its texts include the Vājasaneyi Saṃhitā, Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa, and Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad; represented here as a catalogue entry.
