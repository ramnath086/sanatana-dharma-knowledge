The principal Vedic deities: Agni (fire), Indra (rain/warrior), Varuṇa (cosmic order), Mitra (contracts), the Aśvins (healing), Sūrya/Savitṛ (sun), Uṣas (dawn), Vāyu (wind), Rudra (storm), Viṣṇu (strides), Pūṣan (paths), Bṛhaspati (priesthood); their hymns, functions, and evolution into later Hindu deities are represented here as a catalogue entry.
