Gandhari (गांधारी) is the blind Kaurva queen, wife of Dhṛtarāṣṭra, who voluntarily blinded herself to share her husband's condition. The Śānti Parva appoints her the repository of statecraft (rājādharma) and lamentation; the Vidura Smṛti and the Gandhārī-dhyāna preserve her precepts. She is mother of the hundred Kauravas and the voice of dynastic continuity; her lament for the war is among the epic's most moving passages.
