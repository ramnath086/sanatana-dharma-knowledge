Yudhiṣṭhira (युधिष्ठिर) the 'firm-in-battle', eldest of the Pāṇḍavas (son of Dharma), is the 'righteous king' (dharma-rāja) whose truthfulness earned him the epithet 'the truthful one'. He plays the fatal dice (dyūta) and loses the kingdom, wife, and brothers — the cause of the war. The Mahābhārata (Vana and Śānti Parvas) records his counsel and the 'test' (parikṣā) of his virtue; after the war he ascends the throne and rules the 'age of truth' (satya-yuga), the father of the new lineage.
