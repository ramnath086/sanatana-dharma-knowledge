The forms of Hanumān (Añjanīputra, Māruti, Vāyusuta): the five-faced (pañcamukha) Hanumān, the eleven Rudra manifestations, the vīra (heroic) and dāsya (servitude) aspects; his iconography ( gadā, mountain, saṅjīvanī), his role in Rāmāyaṇa and Mahābhārata, and his worship across India are represented here as a catalogue entry.
