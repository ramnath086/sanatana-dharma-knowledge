The Tantra Śāstra corpus: the textual tradition of Tantra (Śākta, Śaiva, Vaiṣṇava, Buddhist), characterized by ritual (kriyā), meditation (yoga), mantra, yantra, maṇḍala, and the guru-śiṣya transmission.
