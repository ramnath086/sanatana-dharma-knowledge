The Gāyatrī (गायत्री) is the celebrated Vedic metre (twenty-four syllables of the Ṛg Veda 3.62.10) and its personified deity, invoked in the sandhyā of the twice-initiated. The Gāyatrī-mantra — oṃ bhūr bhuvanam... bhargo devasya dhīmahi — is the essence of the sandhyā-upāsanā and the Āraṇyaka, the pivot of brāhmaṇa ritual and the discipline of the atma-yajña. Recited with regulated breath and taught by the preceptor, it is the first mantra imparted to the initiated disciple.
