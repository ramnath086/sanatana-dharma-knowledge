Artha (अर्थ) is material prosperity, power, and worldly security, the second of the four Puruṣārthas and the practical ground of social order. In the Dharmaśāstras artha is to be gained by righteous means (dharma-saṃmukha), pursued through the householder stage (gṛhastha āśrama) as the engine of society and the support of the sacred (yajña, dāna). Kautilya's Arthaśāstra treats artha as the science of polity and wealth, but subordinated to dharma. The Gītā subordinates artha to dharma: dharmārtha as the first consideration of action, while affirming the legitimacy of worldly engagement for the karmayogī.
