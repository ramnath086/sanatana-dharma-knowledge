A folio (pakṣa) is one leaf of a manuscript, a physical artefact with two pages: recto (turned side) and verso (hair side). In Sanskrit palm-leaf manuscripts the folio is oriented vertically, paginated with red or black numerals, and sewn into quires (chhāyas); the term also denotes the folio-numbering system (e.g. 'folio 23 recto'). Catalogues (Muktabodha, Digital Index) record dimensions, condition, and foliation; the digital surrogate preserves each folio with IIIF deep-zoom so readers may inspect scribal corrections, stains, and marginalia without handling the original.
