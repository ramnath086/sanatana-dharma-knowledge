Tantra: the esoteric ritual and yogic tradition transmitted through guru-śiṣya lineages, characterized by the use of mantra, yantra, maṇḍala, dīkṣā (initiation), and the worship of deities in their fierce and benign forms; its textual corpus (Tantras, Āgamas, Saṃhitās), its distinction from Vedic smārta practice, and its influence on temple ritual, iconography, and popular practice are represented here as a catalogue entry.
