The twenty-eight principal Śaiva Āgamas (e.g., Kāmika, Kāraṇa, Suprabheda, Raurava, Mṛgendra, Sarvokta, Pārameśvara, Kiraṇa, Vātula, Parameśvara) and their upāgamas; their fourfold structure (jñāna, yoga, kriyā, caryā), their doctrine of Śiva-Śakti, the pāśa (bondage) and mokṣa; their role in temple construction, daily worship, and initiation are represented here as a catalogue entry.
