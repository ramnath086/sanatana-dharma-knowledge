Numismatics is the study of coins (Tāmra-śāstra) and other monetary and stamp impressions (tāmra-patra) as historical and economic evidence. In India the field spans punch-marked coins (1st millennium BCE) through the silver karshapanas of the Gupta age, the gold dinars of the Gurjara-Pratihara and the Chola, and the inscribed copper-plates (tāmra-patra) recording land grants. Numismatic evidence dates texts and monuments, reveals trade networks, and identifies issuer-legends (devanām pāla-karaṇḍavaha). Catalogues (Mukherjee, Singh) and hoard-studies anchor regional chronologies.
