Dharma (धर्म) is a core Sanskritic concept whose meanings range from duty, law, and righteousness to the intrinsic nature or essence of a thing. In the Vedas dharma is coeval with cosmic order (Ṛta); in the Dharmaśāstras it becomes the regulatory norm governing duty by āśrama and varṇa; in the Buddhist teachings it denotes the teachings and the constituents of experience. The term is context-sensitive: svadharma (one's own duty) in the Gītā, mānuṣadharma (human duty), and sanātana dharma (eternal order) represent later Indian-religious usage. Dharma operates in dynamic tension with artha, kāma, and mokṣa (the four puruṣārthas), and its specification is a central concern across the Darśanas.
