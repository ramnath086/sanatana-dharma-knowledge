The Bhagavad Gītā is structured in 18 adhyāyas (chapters), traditionally arranged by topic: the first chapter (Arjauna-viṣāda) presents the battlefield grief; chapters 2–6 cover the yoga of knowledge, action, and meditation (karma-, jñāna-, dhyāna-yoga); chapters 7–12 describe the higher knowledge of the Supreme (para-parigraha) and the yoga of devotion (bhakti-yoga); chapters 13–18 culminate in the instruction on the field (kurukṣetra) covering the field-and-knowing-field (kṣetra-kṣetjñā-viveka), renunciation, and the threefold faith.
