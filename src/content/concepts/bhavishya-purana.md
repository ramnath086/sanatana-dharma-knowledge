The Bhaviṣya Purāṇa, a Mahāpurāṇa in four parts (Brāhma, Madhyama, Pratisarga, Uttara), containing prophecies (bhaviṣya) about future dynasties, the coming of various religious figures, and the Kali Yuga; its historical layers and textual complexity are represented here as a catalogue entry.
