The Prasthāna-traya ('the three sources/canon') is the text-corpus of the Vedānta: the Upaniṣads (Prasthāna, the 'scriptural' source), the Brahma-sūtras (Nyāya, the 'reasoning' source), and the Bhagavad Gītā (Smṛti, the 'traditional' source). Śaṅkara's bhāṣyas on the trio are the foundation of the Advaita system; each Vedānta school (Advaita, Viśiṣṭādvaita, Dvaita, Śuddhādvaita, Gauḍīya) takes the trio as its starting point (prasthāna) and reconciles its teaching.
