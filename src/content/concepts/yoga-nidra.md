Yoga Nidrā (yogic sleep): a guided relaxation and meditation technique leading to a state of conscious deep sleep between waking and sleeping; its roots in the tantric tradition (nyāsa), its modern systematization (Satyananda Saraswati), and its therapeutic applications are represented here as a catalogue entry.
