Bhīṣma (भीष्म) the 'terrible one', grandson of Śāntanu and the Ganga, is the grandsire of the Kuru dynasty, famed for his lifelong oath (vrata) of service to the throne on the bed of arrows (śāpa-bodha). The Mahābhārata places him in command of the Kuru army against the Pāṇḍavas; his discourse on statecraft (rājādharma), the law of war, and the fourfold dharma is recorded in the Śānti and Anuśāsana Parvas. He dies on the bed of arrows (by Karṇa's arrow) and is the archetypal kṣatriya who subordinates personal happiness to the lineage (vansha).
