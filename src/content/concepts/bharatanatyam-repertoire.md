The traditional Bharatanatyam performance repertoire (mārgam): alarippu (invocation), jatisvaram (pure dance), śabdam (lyric dance), varṇam (centerpiece), padam (abhinaya), tillāṇa (finale), and ślokam/maṅgalam (conclusion).
