Durga (दुर्गा) is the fierce form of the Goddess who battles the buffalo-demon Mahiṣasura, as told in the Mārcarākṣa of the Devi-Māhābhārata and the Devi-Bhāgavata. She rides a lion and holds the discus, mace, lotus, and conch, and is worshipped at the autumn (Āsvina) festival of Durga Puja as the victory of consciousness over ego. Regional forms include Kālī and Badrakālī; her iconography is codified in dhyāna-slokas and she is the supreme Śakti worshiped across the goddess (Śākta) tradition.
