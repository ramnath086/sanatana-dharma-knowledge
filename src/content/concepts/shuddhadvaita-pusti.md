In Śuddhādvaita Vedānta (Vallabha), puṣṭi (grace) is the unmerited divine favor that enables the soul's participation in Kṛṣṇa's līlā; the puṣṭi-mārga (path of grace) and its distinction from the maryādā-mārga (path of law) are represented here as a catalogue entry.
