The Mahāmṛtyuñjaya Mantra — tryambakaṃ yajāmahe... sarvapāpeha (Ṛg Veda 7.59.12 to Tryambaka) — is the pre-eminent Śaiva invocation for longevity, healing, and deliverance from untimely death. It is recited in the Rudra-abhiṣeka on Mahaśiva Rātri and is the 'king of hymns' (sūkta-rajju). The Gauḍīya tradition pairs it with the Hare-Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra as a sādhana of heart's deliverance (hṛdaya-mukti); it is the verse of the Sandarbhas (kāyānta).
