The Hindustani gharanā (lineage/school) system: the major vocal gharanās (Gwalior, Agra, Jaipur-Atrauli, Kirana, Patiala, Bhendi Bazaar, Indore, Mewati, Rampur-Sahaswan) and instrumental gharanās (Imdadkhani/Etawah sitar, Maihar sarod, etc.); the guru-śiṣya paramparā, distinctive gayaki/baaj (style), and their historical development are represented here as a catalogue entry.
