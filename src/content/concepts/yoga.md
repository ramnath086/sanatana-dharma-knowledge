Yoga (योग) is the discipline (yuj, 'to yoke') of the self (ātman) for the cessation of the mind's modifications (citta-vṛtti-nirodha). The Yoga of Patañjali (Yoga-sūtras) is the 'eight-limbed' (ashtanga): yama, niyama, āsana, prāṇāyāma, pratyāhāra, dhāraṇā, dhyāna, samādhi. The Gītā teaches the 'three' (tripartite) yoga — karma (action), jñāna (knowledge), and bhakti (devotion) — as convergent (samanvaya) means to the one goal (para). Yoga is both the theory (śāstra) and the practice (abhyāsa) of the self's purification and union.
