Vidura (विदुर) is the wise elder and advisor of the Kuru court, the son of the servant and sage Dharu, whose counsel guides Yudhiṣṭhira and the state through the moral crises of the Mahābhārata. The Vidura Smṛti (Vidurasmṛti) and the Draupadi-sabha preserve his precepts on statecraft (rājanītikathā) and the duties of rulers (rājadharma). His voice of moderation and his birth transcending jāti make him the exemplar of the wise minister (amātya) whose virtue exceeds birth.
