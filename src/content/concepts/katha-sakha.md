The Kaṭha Śākhā is a recension of the Kṛṣṇa Yajurveda; its texts include the Kaṭha Saṃhitā, Brāhmaṇa, Āraṇyaka, and the Kaṭha Upaniṣad; represented here as a catalogue entry.
