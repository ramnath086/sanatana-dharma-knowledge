Varṇa (वर्ण) is the traditional fourfold division of society — Brāhmaṇa, Kṣatriya, Vaiśya, Śūdra — rooted in the Ṛg Vedic Puruṣa Sūkta and elaborated in the Dharmaśāstras and the Jātaka stories. Varṇa is classically distinguished from jāti (birth-community), which multiplied far beyond the fourfold scheme; the Buddhist critique of varṇa as a basis for social hierarchy is found in the Aṅguttaranikāya. In the Gītā (4.13) the Lord declares varṇaṃ ... guṇaiḥ — the fourfold order as a functional taxonomy of gunas, not a rigid birth-ascriptive hierarchy. Modern scholarship treats varṇa as an idealized model rather than historical reality.
