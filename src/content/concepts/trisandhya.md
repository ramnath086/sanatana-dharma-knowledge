The trisandhyā (three daily junctions): the Vedic prescription for worship at dawn (prātaḥ-sandhyā), noon (mādhyāhnika), and dusk (sāyaṃ-sandhyā); the Gāyatrī mantra recitation, prāṇāyāma, arghya offering, and upasthāna; its role in the daily routine of the twice-born (dvija) and its continuation in modern practice are represented here as a catalogue entry.
