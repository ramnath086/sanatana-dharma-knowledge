The recensions (śākhās) of the Yajurveda: Kṛṣṇa (Black) Yajurveda śākhās — Taittirīya, Maitrāyaṇīya, Kaṭha, Kāpiṣṭhala — and Śukla (White) Yajurveda śākhās — Mādhyandina and Kāṇva; their structural differences (Saṃhitā/Brāhmaṇa separation vs. intermingling) are represented here as a catalogue entry.
