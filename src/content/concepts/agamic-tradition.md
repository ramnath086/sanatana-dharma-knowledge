The Āgamic (Tantric) tradition: the Śaiva Āgamas (28 principal texts), Vaiṣṇava Pāñcarātras and Vaikhānasas, Śākta Tantras; their structure (jñāna, yoga, kriyā, caryā), their role in temple ritual, iconography, and initiation (dīkṣā); their distinction from Vedic orthopraxy and their integration into Smārta tradition are represented here as a catalogue entry.
