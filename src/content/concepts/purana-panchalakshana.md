The five characteristic topics (pañcalakṣaṇa) that define a Purāṇa: sarga (primary creation), pratisarga (secondary creation), vaṃśa (genealogies), manvantara (cosmic cycles), and vaṃśānucarita (dynastic histories); their textual manifestation across the Purāṇic corpus is represented here as a catalogue entry.
