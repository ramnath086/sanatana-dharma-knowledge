The Vaiśeṣika atomic theory positing four kinds of eternal, indivisible atoms (paramāṇu) — earth, water, fire, air — each with distinct qualities (guṇa); their binary combinations (dvyanuka) and ternary combinations (tryanuka) forming the gross physical world; the theory's differences from Greek atomism and its role in Vaiśeṣika physics are represented here as a catalogue entry.
