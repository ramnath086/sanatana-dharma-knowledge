Digitization is the production of digital surrogates of manuscripts and inscriptions: high-resolution spectral (multi-/multi-spectral) imaging, metadata capture (Dublin Core, MODS), and optical character recognition (OCR/HTR) for scripts such as Devanāgarī, Grantha, and Tamil. Standards (FADGI 4-star, Metamorfoze, UNESCO) govern image quality and metadata; workflows include multispectral imaging of faded ink, crowdsourced transcription (e.g. via Wikisource), and IIIF delivery. The Digital India Library and Muktabodha host digitized Sanskrit texts and manuscripts; access must honor source copyright and cultural sensitivities.
