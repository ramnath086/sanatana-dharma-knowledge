Prayāsa-cchita (प्रायश्चित्त) is the Dharmaśāstra genre of expiatory rites for intentional or incidental transgressions, performed by the 'means of cleansing' (√yaj, √śam). The Smṛtis enumerate the expiations: penance (tapas), bathing (snāna), feeding the Brāhmana (anna-dāna), recitation of the Gāyatrī, and the 'great' expiations (e.g. three days of fasting). Their form (karma) varies by caste (varṇa) and by the severity of the offense; the Gītā (4.13) frames expiation as the restoration of dharma.
