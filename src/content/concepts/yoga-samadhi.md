The Yoga concept of samādhi (absorption): the stages of saṃprajñāta (with cognitive content — vitarka, vicāra, ānanda, asmitā) and asaṃprajñāta (without cognitive content, nirbīja); the distinction between sabīja (with seed) and nirbīja (seedless) samādhi, and the role of dispassion (vairāgya) and practice (abhyāsa) are represented here as a catalogue entry.
