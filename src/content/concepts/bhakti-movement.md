The Bhakti movement (7th–17th centuries): the devotional renaissance across India emphasizing personal devotion (bhakti) to a chosen deity (iṣṭa-devatā) over ritual orthodoxy; its poet-saints (Āḻvārs, Nāyanārs, Vārkarīs, Haridāsas, Rāmānanda, Kabīr, Tulsīdās, Mīrābāī, Sūrdās, Caitanya); its vernacular literature, social egalitarianism, and lasting impact on Hindu practice are represented here as a catalogue entry.
