Collation is the systematic comparison of manuscript (or print) witnesses to identify and record their agreements and disagreements, the first stage of establishing a critical text. Manual collation (by eye, against a copy-text), mechanical collation (19th-c. comparators), and digital collation (CollateX, Juxta, e-Annotated) produce variant matrices. The result feeds the critical apparatus: witnesses are grouped into text-types (recensions) and weighted by age, provenance, and fidelity to the archetype, enabling stemmatic or eclectic reconstruction of the most probable original reading.
