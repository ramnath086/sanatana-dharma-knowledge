Prakṛti (प्रकृति) is primordial matter or nature, the source of the material universe in Sāṃkhya and the field (kṣetra) of Puruṣa-consciousness in the Gītā (13.19–20). From the equilibrium of the three Guṇas (sattva, rajas, tamas) Prakṛti differentiates into the Mahābhūtas (five great elements) and from them the ten Mahābhūtas-digāma indriyas, forming the subtle and gross bodies that bind Puruṣa. Kālāmsa's Sāṃkhyakārikā defines Prakṛti as the matrix of the twenty-five tattvas (mahābhūta up to abuddha). Liberation (mokṣa) is the cessation of this material manifestation through the discriminative knowledge (vivekanābhyāsa) that Puruṣa is distinct from Prakṛti.
