The light classical forms of Hindustani music: thumrī (romantic, erotic, based on Braj/Hindi poetry, set to dīpacandī/punjabi tāl), dadra (lighter, faster, in dadra tāl), kājrī, caitī, horī; their gharanā styles (Purab, Punjab), their place in the concert format after khayāl, and their emotive (śṛṅgāra) content are represented here as a catalogue entry.
