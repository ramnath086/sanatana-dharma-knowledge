The Kāpiṣṭhala Śākha (or Kāṭhaka) is a lesser recension (śākha) of the Black (Kṛṣṇa) Yajur Veda, closely related to the Kāṭha Śākha and known from the Kāpiṣṭhala Gṛhya-sūtra and an Āraṇyaka, both partially preserved. It is one of the surviving śākhas of the Yajur Veda and is cited in the Dharmaśāstras for certain life-cycle rites (e.g. the sacred-thread ceremony).
