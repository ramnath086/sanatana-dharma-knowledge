In Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta, prapatti (śaraṇāgati) is the act of complete self-surrender to Viṣṇu as the sole means to liberation; its six limbs (ānikūlya-varjana, ānukūlya-saṅkalpa, rakṣiṣyatīti-viśvāsa, goptṛtva-varaṇa, ātma-nikṣepa, kārpaṇya) are represented here as a catalogue entry.
