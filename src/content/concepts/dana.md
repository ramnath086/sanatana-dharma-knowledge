Dāna (दान) is the practice of charitable giving, a key householder duty (karma) and one of the principal means (puruṣārtha) of social merit. The Dharmaśāstras (Manu 4.138, Yājñavalkya 1.258) distinguish the four kinds of gift (amṛta-dāna, dhānya-dāna, jñāna-dāna, and dāna to Brāhmanas), and the Gītā (17.20) grades giving by the giver's motive (sattva, rajas, tamas). The gift (dāna) is the 'eye of the giver' in Buddhist and Jain ethics and a central śrāddha (ancestor) practice.
