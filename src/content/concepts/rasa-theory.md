Rasa (रस) is the aesthetic sentiment, the 'flavour' evoked in the audience by art, systematized in the Nāṭyaśāstra as the eight primary rasas: śṛṅgāra (love), hāsya (humour), karuṇa (compassion), raudra (anger), vīra (heroism), bhayānaka (terror), bībhatsa (disgust), adbhuta (wonder); with śānta added in the later tradition. The theory (rasa-siddhānta), developed by Abhinavagupta, is the aesthetic counterpart to devotion (bhāva); rasa is the relish (āsvāda) of the fully-experienced emotion. The conditions (ālambana, ātmikā, vibhāvya) and the nine rasas structure classical Sanskrit poetics and the performing arts (nṛtya).
