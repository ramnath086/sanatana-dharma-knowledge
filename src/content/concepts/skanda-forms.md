The forms of Skanda (Kārttikeya, Murugaṇ, Subrahmaṇya): the six-faced Ṣaṇmukha, the youthful Kumāra, the warrior Senāpati, and the Tamil Murugaṇ; their iconography (vel, peacock, rooster) and regional worship (especially Tamil Nadu) are represented here as a catalogue entry.
