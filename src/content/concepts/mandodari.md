Māndodarī (मांदोदरी) is the chaste queen of Rāvaṇa in the Rāmāyaṇa, the 'wide-bellied' (maudodarya) queen of Laṅkā and the voice of conscience to her husband. The Sundara Kāṇḍa records her warning (the 'consequences of war' against Rāma) and her lament at the death of her son Indrajit (Meghanāḍa). She is the archetype of the wise and patient queen (rāṇī) and the faithful wife (patnī), and her counsel that 'Rāma's victory is at hand' marks the turning of the Lanka-episode.
