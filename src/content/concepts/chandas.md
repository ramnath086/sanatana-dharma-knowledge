Chandas (prosody) is the Vedāṅga governing the quantitative and accentual metre of Vedic and classical Sanskrit verse. It classifies metres by gaṇa (syllable-groups) and vṛttas (lines of equal length), the fundamental schemes being the Gayātri (24 syllables, 8+8+8), Anuṣṭubh (4×8), and the jāti metres of the Ṛg Veda. The canonical text is Pingala's Chandaḥśāstra; the discipline also comprises the three-syllable weight analysis (laghu, guru, pūrṇa) and the rules of yamaka, anuprāsa, and other figures of speech (alaṅkāra-vāda). Mastery of chandas is prerequisite for correct Vedic recitation and the aesthetics of classical kāvya.
