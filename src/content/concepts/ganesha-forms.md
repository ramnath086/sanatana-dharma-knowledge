The forms of Gaṇeśa: Bāl Gaṇeśa, Taruṇa Gaṇeśa, Bhakti Gaṇeśa, Vīra Gaṇeśa, Śakti Gaṇeśa, Dvija Gaṇeśa, Siddhi Gaṇeśa, Ucchiṣṭa Gaṇeśa, Vighna Gaṇeśa, Kṣipra Gaṇeśa, Heramba Gaṇeśa, and Lakṣmī Gaṇeśa; their iconographic variations and the thirty-two forms tradition are represented here as a catalogue entry.
