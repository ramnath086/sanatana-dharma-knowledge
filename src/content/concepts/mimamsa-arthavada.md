The Mīmāṃsā theory of arthavāda (explanatory passages in the Veda): the classification of Vedic sentences into vidhi (injunction), nṣedha (prohibition), and arthavāda (explanation); the three types of arthavāda — guṇavāda (eulogy), anuvāda (restatement), bhūtārthavāda (statement of fact) — and their hermeneutical role in ritual interpretation are represented here as a catalogue entry.
