Oṃ Namo Nārāyaṇāya is the traditional eight-syllable aṣṭākṣara invocation of Viṣṇu/Bhagavān, cited in the Ṛg Veda, the Nārāyaṇa Upaniṣad, and the Divya Prabandham. It is the core dhyāna-sādhana of the Vaiṣṇṇava traditions (Gauḍīya, Śrīvaiṣṇava, Dvaita), the heart of the Nārāyaṇa-mantra, and recited in the sandhyā and the Gītā-pūjā. The eight syllables unfold the progression from the embodied self to the supreme Nārāyaṇa, and are the formula of safe conclusion (sukhānta) taught at the deathbed.
