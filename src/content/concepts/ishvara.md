Īśvara (ईश्वर) is the personal Lord, the soteriological ground in the Āstika Darśanas, understood variously as the creator, the author of the Vedas (svayaṃ-śruti), and the object of devotion (bhakti). In Yoga (Patañjali 1.23) Īśvara is a special puruṣa (viśeṣa) free from karma; in Vedānta Īśvara is Brahman immanent as the causal (sarvāntara) and efficient cause. The Gītā (9.2) calls Īśvara the source of the Gītā itself. The Īśvara-praṇidhāna (surrender to the Lord) is a limb of Yoga and a gateway of bhakti.
