Śruti (that which is heard): the authoritative revealed texts of Hinduism, comprising the four Vedas (Ṛg, Yajur, Sāma, Atharva) and their associated Brāhmaṇas, Āraṇyakas, and Upaniṣads; distinguished from Smṛti (remembered tradition); its apauruṣeya (non-human authorship) status, its role as the ultimate pramāṇa in orthodox schools, and its oral transmission (paramparā) are represented here as a catalogue entry.
