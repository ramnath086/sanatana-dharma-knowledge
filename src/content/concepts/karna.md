Karṇa (कर्ण) the 'ear-rings' hero is the forgotten son of Kunti (by Sūrya), raised by the charioteer-couple, and the champion of the Kauravas — the 'friend' of Duryodhana and the foil of Arjuna (his rival in arms and in dharma). The Mahābhārata (Karṇa and Śalya Parvas) depicts his unmatched generosity (dātṛtā) and the 'golden' (kanīnī) ear-rings of Virūpākṣa, his martial prowess (the 'head-to-head' fight with Arjuna), and his death by Arjuna's arrow while mounted (śiraḥ-stabdhāḥ). His tragedy is the tension between birth (jāti) and merit (karma).
