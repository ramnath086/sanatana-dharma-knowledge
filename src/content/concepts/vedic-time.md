Vedic and post-Vedic time cycles: the four yugas (Kṛta, Tretā, Dvāpara, Kali) and their durations (4:3:2:1), the mahāyuga (4.32 million years), the manvantara (71 mahāyugas), the kalpa (1000 mahāyugas = 4.32 billion years), Brahmā's day and night; their Puranic elaboration and traditional cosmological significance are represented here as a catalogue entry.
