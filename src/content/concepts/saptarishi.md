The Saptarṣi (seven sages): the seven primordial sages (Marīci, Atri, Aṅgiras, Pulastya, Pulaha, Kratu, Vasiṣṭha) who are the mind-born sons of Brahmā, the authors of Vedic hymns, and the progenitors of gotras; their astronomical identification with the Ursa Major constellation (Saptarṣi Maṇḍala) and their role in Vedic tradition are represented here as a catalogue entry.
