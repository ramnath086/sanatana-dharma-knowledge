The Bhagavad Gītā's teaching of karma-yoga (the yoga of selfless action): niṣkāma-karma (action without attachment to fruits), svadharma (one's own duty), and the synthesis of action and knowledge; its exposition across chapters 2–5 is represented here as a catalogue entry.
