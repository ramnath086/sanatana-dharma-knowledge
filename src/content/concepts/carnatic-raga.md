The Carnatic rāga system: the 72 melakartā (parent) rāgas organized in the kaṭapayādi formula, their janya (derived) rāgas; the rāga structure (ārohaṇa/avarohaṇa), the gamaka (ornamentation), and the role of rāga in conveying rasa (emotion) are represented here as a catalogue entry.
