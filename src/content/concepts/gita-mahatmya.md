The Gītā-mahātmya ('glorification of the Gītā') is the genre of Purāṇic hymns extolling the Bhagavad Gītā as the foremost of spiritual science. The best-known (Vallabha's Bhāgavata-related version, the Anuvābhāṣikī) declares the Gītā superior to the Vedas and the Gītā-dhyāna of Sūta. These Mahātmyas are recited in the Gītā-purṇa-kathā and on Gītā-jayanti, and they encode a theological hierarchy (Īśvara → Gītā → guru → text). The Gītā-mahātmya literature thus mediates between scriptural authority and devotional practice, securing the text's ritual performance (patha, homa).
