A siglum (abbreviated witness identifier) is a symbolic label used in a critical apparatus to designate a manuscript or edition (e.g. V for the Vulgate, Gk for a Greek Gospel manuscript, or 'M' for the Mackenzie manuscript of a Sanskrit work). Modern critical editions follow conventions (CMG, LLA) assigning unique sigla and grouping witnesses into text-families (recensions) so that the apparatus records, with each lemma, the witnesses supporting every attested variant. The Digital Critical Edition Consortium (DCEC) is extending siglum schemes to digital corpora.
