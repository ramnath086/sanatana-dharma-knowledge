The Bhāgavata Purāṇa (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam) has attracted a major commentarial tradition: Śrīdhara Svāmī (14th c., Bhāvārthadīpikā Bhāṣya), Śaṅkara-deva, and the Vaiṣṇṇava exegetes Jīva Gosvāmī (Sat-khyā-tīdyā), Viśvanātha Cakravartī (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatamṛta, 1705), and Radhāmohan Gosvāmī. Their commentaries defend the Bhāgavata's primacy (muktāṅga-tantra) and reconcile its myths with Vedānta; Sat-sandarbhas and the Gopāla-campūṣaṇa are accessory. The critical edition (BORI/VA) and the Motilal Banarsidass edition supply the text for modern study.
