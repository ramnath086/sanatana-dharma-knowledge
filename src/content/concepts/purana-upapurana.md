The eighteen Upapurāṇas (minor Purāṇas): Saura, Nṛsiṃha, Viṣṇu Dharmottara, Kālikā, Garga, Āditya, Nandi, Sāmba, Nṛsiṃha Tāpanīya, Bhārgava, Matsya, Varāha, Kapila, Mānava, Auśanasa, Sāmba, Nṛsiṃha Tāpanīya, and Bhārgava; their relationship to the Mahāpurāṇas and their distinct thematic foci are represented here as a catalogue entry.
