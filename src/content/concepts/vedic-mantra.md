The classification of Vedic mantras: ṛc (Ṛgveda verses), yajus (Yajurveda prose), sāman (Sāmaveda chants), nigada (loud recitation); the division into mantra and brāhmaṇa portions; the gāyatrī, uṣṇik, anuṣṭubh, bṛhatī, paṅkti, triṣṭubh, jagatī metres; the distinction between mantra and bhāṣya are represented here as a catalogue entry.
