Draupadi (द्रौप्य) is the Pāṇḍava wife, central to the Mahābhārata's central moral-legal crisis. The Dice Game in the Sabhā Parva, in which she is wagered and humiliated, triggers the war. Her vow never to bind her hair keeps it loose until the war's end. Her son Abhimanyu's birth and death frame the middle of the epic, and her later lament anchors the fall of the Kurus. She is the archetype of dharma under duress and the exemplary patient wife (pativrata).
