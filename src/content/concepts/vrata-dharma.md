The role of vrata (vowed observance) in Dharmaśāstra: the classification into nitya (daily), naimittika (occasion-specific), and kāmya (desire-motivated); the components of vrata (saṅkalpa, upavāsa, japa, dāna, pūjā); its role in purification, merit (puṇya), and the attainment of specific goals are represented here as a catalogue entry.
