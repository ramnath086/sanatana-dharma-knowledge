The Kāṇḍas (books) of the Vālmiki Rāmāyaṇa are seven: Bāla, Ayodhyā, Āraṇya, Kiṣkindhā, Sundara, Yuddha, and Uttara (the later). The Bāla tells Rāma's birth; Ayodhya the exile (bhrammaṇa); Āraṇya the forest (vana-vāsa); Kiṣkindhā the search (siddha) with Hanumān and the vanaras; Sundara the finding (sphurana) of Sītā in Lanka; Yuddha the war (the 'great war' of Rāma vs. Rāvaṇa); Uttara Rāma's coronation and the birth of the next generation (Lava-Kūla and the Rāma-setu).
