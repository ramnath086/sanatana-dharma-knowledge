The recensions (śākhās) of the Atharvaveda: Śaunaka (the principal surviving recension) and Paippalāda (partially preserved in Kashmir and Orissa); their textual differences and the Atharvaveda's unique character among the four Vedas are represented here as a catalogue entry.
