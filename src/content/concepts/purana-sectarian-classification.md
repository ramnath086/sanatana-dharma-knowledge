The traditional tripartite classification of Purāṇas by predominance of guṇas: Sāttvika (Viṣṇu/Kṛṣṇa), Rājasa (Brahmā), and Tāmasa (Śiva); this classification's textual basis and sectarian implications are represented here as a catalogue entry.
