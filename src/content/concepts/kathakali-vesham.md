The Kathakali vesham (makeup/costume) classification: paccā (green, noble/heroic), kaṭṭi (knife, anti-heroic), kari (black, demonic), teṭṭa (beard, ascetic), minukku (polished, female/sage); the symbolic colors, the chutti (white ridge), and the elaborate headgear (kireetam) are represented here as a catalogue entry.
