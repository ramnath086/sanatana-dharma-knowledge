In Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta, nitya-vibhūti (eternal manifestation) is the spiritual realm of Vaikuṇṭha, distinct from the material world (līlā-vibhūti); its nature as the abode of the released souls and the eternal sport of Viṣṇu is represented here as a catalogue entry.
