Lakṣmāṇa (लक्ष्मण) is the younger brother and constant companion of Rāma in the Vālmiki Rāmāyaṇa, the embodiment of faithful service (sevaka-dharma). He builds the bridge (setu-bandha) to Lanka, guards Sītā in the forest, and is the interlocutor of the Yuddha-kāṇḍa's counsel. He is worshipped as a patron of learning, and his loyalty (niyati) and resourcefulness (upāya) are the model of the ideal companion (saṃgāyitr) in epic and literature.
