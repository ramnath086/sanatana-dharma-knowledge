The system of Vedic metres (chandas): gāyatrī (24 syllables), uṣṇik (28), anuṣṭubh (32), bṛhatī (36), paṅkti (40), triṣṭubh (44), jagatī (48), and their use in the Ṛgveda Saṃhitā; the transition to classical Sanskrit metres is represented here as a catalogue entry.
