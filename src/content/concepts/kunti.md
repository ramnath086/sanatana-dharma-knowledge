Kunti (कुन्ती) is the Pāṇḍava mother, a Yādava princess married to the blind king Dhṛtarāṣṭra's brother, and foster-mother to Kṛṣṇa. The epic's early section recounts her boon — Kṛṣṇa as her son — and her later reunion with Karna, her firstborn. The Kunti-stotra and the Karṇa-parva preserve her voice of maternal grief and resolve; her patience (dhairya) and devotion to Kṛṣṇa (bhakti) are central themes of the epic's ethics of suffering (duḥkha).
