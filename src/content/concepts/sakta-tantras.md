The Śākta Tantras: the Mahānirvāṇa Tantra, Kulārṇava Tantra, Rudrayāmala, Brahmayāmala, and the Śrīvidyā tradition (Tripurā Rahasya, Nityāṣoḍaśikārṇava); their doctrine of the Goddess as supreme (Śakti/Devī), the śrīcakra, the ten Mahāvidyās, and the kuṇḍalinī yoga; their role in Śrīvidyā upāsanā and temple ritual are represented here as a catalogue entry.
