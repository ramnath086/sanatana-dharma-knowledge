The Yoga concept of viveka-khyāti (discriminative knowledge) as the direct intuitive realization of the distinction between puruṣa (consciousness) and prakṛti (matter); its attainment through the practice of the eight limbs (aṣṭāṅga) leading to kaivalya (liberation); its distinction from mere intellectual understanding are represented here as a catalogue entry.
