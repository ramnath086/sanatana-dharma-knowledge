The five great elements (pañcamahābhūta) in Vedic and Sāṃkhya cosmology: ākāśa (space/ether), vāyu (air), agni (fire), jala/ap (water), pṛthvī (earth); their subtle essences (tanmātras: śabda, sparśa, rūpa, rasa, gandha), their sequence of manifestation, and their correspondence to sense organs are represented here as a catalogue entry.
