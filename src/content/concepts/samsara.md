Saṃsāra (संसार) is the cyclic process of birth, death, and rebirth, the condition of embodied existence (saṃ+√sṝ) driven by karma and the subtle body (liṅga), the soteriological 'background' of all Indian traditions. Its defining marks are impermanence (anitya), suffering (duḥkha), and non-self (anattā) in Buddhism; the three guṇas (sattva, rajas, tamas) in Sāṃkhya-Yoga; and the 'wheel' (cakra) of becoming in the Buddhist dharmacakra. Liberation (mokṣa/nirvāṇa) is the 'blowing out' (nirodha) of this wandering.
