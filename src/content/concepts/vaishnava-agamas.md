The Vaiṣṇava Āgamas: the Pāñcarātra corpus (e.g., Jayākhya, Ahirbudhnya, Paushkara, Sāttvata) and the Vaikhānasa texts; their doctrine of Viṣṇu's five forms (para, vyūha, vibhava, antaryāmin, arcā), the fivefold daily worship (pañcakāla), and their influence on temple ritual in Śrīvaiṣṇava and other traditions are represented here as a catalogue entry.
