The Navagraha ('nine planets' / 'nine celestial influencers') are the group of celestial bodies whose motion governs human affairs (karmānukūla) in traditional Vedic astrology (Jyotiṣa). The nine are: Sūrya (Sun), Candra (Moon), Mangala (Mars), Budha (Mercury), Guru (Jupiter), Śukra (Venus), Śanai (Saturn), plus the shadow-lords Rāhu and Ketu (the lunar nodes). Sūrya is placed first among them as the visible 'source of light'. The Navagraha are worshipped together in homa and temple shrines (often a single 'Navagraha' shrine) and the term also names a category of temple reliefs and devotional texts (Navagraha-kāvya / Naṭa-rājāya).
