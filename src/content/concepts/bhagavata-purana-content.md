The Bhāgavata Purāṇa (Śrīmad Bhāgavatam), its twelve skandhas, focus on Kṛṣṇa-bhakti, the daśāvatāra, and the dialogue between Parīkṣit and Śuka; its theological emphasis on bhakti-yoga is represented here as a catalogue entry.
