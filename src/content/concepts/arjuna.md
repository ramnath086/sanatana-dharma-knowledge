Arjuna (अर्जुन) is the third of the Pāṇḍava brothers, a Mahāratha archer and the interlocutor of the Bhagavad Gītā. The Mahābhārata (Vana, Vāra, Karṇa, and other Parvas) recounts his deeds: the burning of the Khandava forest with Kṛṣṇa, his conquests, and his role as the charioteer addressed by the Gītā's teaching. He is father of Abhimanyu by Subhadrā, and his descendants receive the succession of Hastinākṣa. As a character he embodies the warrior's dilemma of duty (dharma) versus kin, resolved under Kṛṣṇa's instruction.
