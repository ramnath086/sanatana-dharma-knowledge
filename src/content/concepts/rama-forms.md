The forms of Rāma: the prince of Ayodhyā, the exiled forest-dweller, the victor over Rāvaṇa, the ideal king (Rāmarājya), and the supreme Parabrahman (in Adhyātma Rāmāyaṇa); his iconography (bow, arrow, accompanied by Sītā, Lakṣmaṇa, Hanumān), and his worship in the Rāmānandī and Rāmānuja traditions are represented here as a catalogue entry.
