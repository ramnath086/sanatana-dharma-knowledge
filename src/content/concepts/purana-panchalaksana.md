The Pañcalakṣaṇa ('five marks') are the defining characteristics by which a text is recognized as a Purāṇa: (1) Sarga — creation and emanation of beings; (2) Pratisarga — re-creation or the destruction and renewed activity of the cosmos; (3) Vaṃśa — genealogies of kings (solar and lunar) and of the ādikartas; (4) Manvantara — the reigns of the fourteen Manu-s, and the avatāras of Viṣṇu; (5) Vaṃśānucarita — the deeds of the royal dynasties (e.g., the Raghu-vaṃśa). These five topoi organize the narrative frame of the Mahāpurāṇa corpus and distinguish Purāṇic from Mahābhārata and itihāsa composition styles.
