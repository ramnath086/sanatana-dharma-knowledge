Videhamukti (वैदेहमुक्ति, 'liberation in the body-less') is liberation after death — the 'great' (parama) release that occurs when the body falls — as distinct from jīvanmukti (liberation while living). The term 'Videha' (the 'body-less one') denotes the liberated who, freed of the body (deha) and the cycle (saṃsāra), enters the 'pure' (kevala) state. The Advaita distinguishes this 'highest word' (parama-śabda) from the empirical (vyavahārika); the Gītā (8.15–16) describes the 'liberated' who return as the light (jyoti) of the supreme.
