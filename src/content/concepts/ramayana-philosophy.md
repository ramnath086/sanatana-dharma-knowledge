The philosophical and ethical teachings of the Rāmāyaṇa: the ideals of dharma (righteousness), satya (truth), kṣamā (forgiveness), dayā (compassion), and the ideal ruler (Rāma-rājya); the roles of ideal son, brother, husband, and king as embodied by Rāma, Lakṣmaṇa, Bharata, and Śatrughna are represented here as a catalogue entry.
