The Dvaita Vedānta doctrine of five eternal differences (pañca-bheda): between Brahman (Viṣṇu) and jīva, between Brahman and jagat, between jīva and jīva, between jīva and jagat, and between jagat and jagat; their epistemic and ontological status are represented here as a catalogue entry.
