The types of śrāddha (ancestral rites) in Dharmaśāstra: nitya (daily), naimittika (occasional), kāmya (desire-motivated), and the specific rites like sapiṇḍīkaraṇa, saparvaṇa, ekodiṣṭa; their performers, timing, and offerings are represented here as a catalogue entry.
