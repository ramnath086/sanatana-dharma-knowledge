Viṭṭhala (Vithoba, Paṇḍurāṅga), the deity of Pandharpur, Maharashtra, a form of Viṣṇu/Kṛṣṇa standing on a brick (viṭ); the Vārkarī tradition, the annual Palkhi pilgrimage from Alandi and Dehu, the abhangas of Jñāneśvar and Tukārām; his dark complexion, hands on hips posture, and the Chandrabhaga river are represented here as a catalogue entry.
