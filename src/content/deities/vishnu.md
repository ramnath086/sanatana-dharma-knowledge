Viṣṇu (विष्णु / விஷ்ணு / ವಿಷ್ಣು / വിഷ്ണു / বিষ্ণુ / વિષ્ણು), the All-Pervading One, Nārāyaṇa, Hari, Kṛṣṇa, Rāma, Vāsudeva, Govinda, Madhava, Keśava. Central to Vaiṣṇava traditions (Śrīvaiṣṇavism, Gauḍīya, Puṣṭimārga, Nimbārka, Madhva). Ten principal avatāras (daśāvatāra): Matsya, Kūrma, Varāha, Narasiṃha, Vāmana, Paraśurāma, Rāma, Kṛṣṇa, Buddha, Kalki. Consort: Lakṣmī/Śrī. Abode: Vaikuṇṭha. Vāhana: Garuḍa. Attributes: Śaṅkha (conch), Cakra (discus), Gada (mace), Padma (lotus). Key texts: Viṣṇu Purāṇa, Bhāgavata Purāṇa, Nārāyaṇīya, Pāñcarātra Āgamas, Nālāyira Divya Prabandham (Tamil). Festivals: Janmāṣṭamī, Rāma-navamī, Vaikuṇṭha Ekādaśī, Onam (Vāmana), Dīpāvalī.
---
A major deity with diverse textual, ritual, and regional forms; this record does not impose a single theology on the many traditions that center on Viṣṇu.
