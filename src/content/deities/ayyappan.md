Ayyappan (അയ്യപ്പന്‍ / ஐயப்பன்), also Śāstā, Hariharaputra, Dharma-Śāstā — the deity of Sabarimala, born from the union of Śiva and Viṣṇu (as Mohinī). Embodies the synthesis of Śaiva and Vaiṣṇava traditions. Key attributes: celibacy (naiṣṭhika brahmacarya), the 18 holy steps (paṭinēṭṭāmpaṭi / பதினெட்டாம்படி), the 41-day vrata (maṇḍala kālam / மண்டல காலம்) with strict observances (black/blue clothes, no shaving, brahmacarya, daily bath). The pilgrimage to Sabarimala (Kerala) draws 40-50 million annually (Nov-Jan). The Makaravilakku (Makara Jyoti) on Makara Saṅkrānti is the climax. Worshipped across Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and beyond. The Vāvaru (Muslim devotee) shrine at Erumeli exemplifies communal harmony.
