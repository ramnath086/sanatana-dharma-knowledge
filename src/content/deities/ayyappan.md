Ayyappan (Śāstā, Hariharaputra), the deity of Sabarimala, born from the union of Śiva and Viṣṇu (as Mohinī); his celibacy (naiṣṭhika brahmacarya), the 18 holy steps (patinettampadi), the 41-day vrata (maṇḍala kālam), the pilgrimage to Sabarimala; his syncretic Śaiva-Vaiṣṇava nature and pan-South Indian popularity are represented here as a catalogue entry.
