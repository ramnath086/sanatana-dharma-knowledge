Narasiṃha, the man-lion avatar of Viṣṇu, whose emergence from the pillar to protect Prahlāda and slay Hiraṇyakaśipu is a central Vaiṣṇava narrative; his iconography, temples, and worship traditions are represented here as a catalogue entry.
