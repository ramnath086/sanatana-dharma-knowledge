Śiva (शिव / சிவன் / ಶಿವ / ശിവന്‍ / শিব / શિવ), the Auspicious One, Maheśvara, Rudra, Paśupati, Nīlakaṇṭha, Naṭarāja (cosmic dancer), Dakṣiṇāmūrti (guru), Ardhanārīśvara (half-female). Central to Śaiva traditions (Śaiva Siddhānta, Kashmir Śaivism, Vīraśaivism, Liṅgāyat). The liṅga (aniconic form) is the primary worship focus. Twelve Jyotirliṅgas. Consort: Pārvatī/Śakti. Sons: Gaṇeśa, Skanda. Vāhana: Nandi (bull). Abode: Kailāsa. Key texts: Śiva Purāṇa, Liṅga Purāṇa, Śaiva Āgamas, Tirumuṟai (Tamil). Festivals: Mahāśivarātri, Pradosha, Kārttikai Tīpam.
---
A major deity with diverse textual, ritual, and regional forms; this record does not impose a single theology on the many traditions that center on Śiva.
