Gaṇeśa (गणेश / விநாயகர் / గణేష్ / ಗಣೇಶ / ഗണപതി / গণেশ / ગણેશ), Vināyaka, Piḷḷaiyār, Vighneśvara, Gajānanā — the elephant-headed deity, remover of obstacles (Vighnahartā), lord of beginnings. Son of Śiva and Pārvatī. Consorts: Siddhi (success), Buddhi (wisdom); or Ṛddhi/Siddhi. Vehicle: Mūṣaka (mouse). Attributes: broken tusk (ekadanta), modaka (sweet), axe, noose. Key texts: Gaṇeśa Purāṇa, Mudgala Purāṇa, Gaṇeśa Atharvaśīrṣa. Worshipped first in all rituals. Festivals: Gaṇeśa Caturthī (Bhādrapada Śukla Caturthī), Vināyaka Caturthī, Saṅkaṣṭī Caturthī (monthly). Regional: Mumbai's Lalbaugcha Raja, Pune's Dagdusheth, Tamil Nadu's Piḷḷaiyārpatti, Kerala's Koṭṭārakkara.
