Madhva's Bhāṣya on the Taittirīya Upaniṣad, interpreting the five kośas as real gradations of being under Viṣṇu's control, the ānanda-mīmāṃsā as establishing hierarchical bliss, and the bhṛgu-vallī as the jīva's progressive service to Viṣṇu.
