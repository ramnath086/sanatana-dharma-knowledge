Śaṅkara's Bhāṣya on the Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad, a later Upaniṣad with strong Śaiva orientation, teaching the unity of Rudra and Brahman, the three guṇas, the wheel of saṃsāra, and the path of devotion (bhakti) to Śiva as the supreme.
