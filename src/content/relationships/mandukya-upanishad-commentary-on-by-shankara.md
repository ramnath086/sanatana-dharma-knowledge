Śaṅkara's Bhāṣya on the Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad (with Gauḍapāda's Kārikā), the shortest Upaniṣad (12 verses), analyzing the four states (waking, dreaming, deep sleep, turīya) and the Oṃkāra as the symbol of Brahman.
