Rāmānuja's Bhāṣya on the Taittirīya Upaniṣad, interpreting the five kośas as real attributes of Brahman, the ānanda-mīmāṃsā as establishing graded bliss in Brahman's manifestations, and the bhṛgu-vallī as the progressive realization of Viṣṇu as the supreme.
