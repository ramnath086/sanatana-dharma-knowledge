Nimbārka's commentary tradition on the Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad from the Bhedābheda (Dvaitādvaita) perspective, teaching the simultaneous difference and non-difference of the jīva and Brahman, with Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa as the supreme couple.
