The Mahākāleśvara Temple at Ujjain is one of the twelve Jyotirliṅgas, dedicated to Śiva as Mahākāla in his fierce form; the temple is unique for its south-facing (dakṣiṇāmukhī) liṅga and the Bhasma Ārati ritual.
