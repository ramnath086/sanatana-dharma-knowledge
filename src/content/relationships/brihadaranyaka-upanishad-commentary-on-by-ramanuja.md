Rāmānuja's Bhāṣya on the Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad from the Viśiṣṭādvaita perspective, interpreting the neti-neti as denying upādhi (limiting adjuncts) not Brahman's attributes, and emphasizing the qualified non-duality of Brahman with cit and acit.
