Vallabha's Bhāṣya on the Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad from the Śuddhādvaita perspective, emphasizing the pure non-duality of Kṛṣṇa as Brahman, the world as His real manifestation (not māyā), and the path of puṣṭi-mārga (grace) through Kṛṣṇa-bhakti.
