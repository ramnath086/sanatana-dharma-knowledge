Rāmānuja's Bhāṣya on the Chāndogya Upaniṣad, interpreting tat tvam archaeological-survey-of-india as the identity of the individual self (jīva) as a mode (prakāra) of Brahman, not absolute identity; the sat-vidyā as establishing Viṣṇu as the supreme Brahman qualified by the world.
