Madhva's Bhāṣya on the Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad from the Dvaita perspective, maintaining the eternal distinction (bheda) between the independent (svatantra) Viṣṇu and the dependent (paratantra) jīvas and matter; the neti-neti negates the world's independence, not its reality as Viṣṇu's creation.
