The Sāmba Purāṇa, one of the eighteen Upapurāṇas, narrated by Sāmba (Kṛṣṇa's son), focusing on Sūrya worship, the healing of Sāmba's leprosy through solar worship, and the solar temples of India; its role in solar traditions is represented here as a catalogue entry.
