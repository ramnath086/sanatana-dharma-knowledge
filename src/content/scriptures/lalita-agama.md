The Lalitā Āgama, one of the twenty-eight principal Śaiva Āgamas, associated with the playful (lalita) aspect of Śiva-Śakti union, integrating Śaiva and Śākta ritual streams in the worship of the Divine Couple; its synthesis orientation is represented here as a catalogue entry.
