The Nārāyaṇa Upaniṣad, a Vaiṣṇava Upaniṣad, identifies Nārāyaṇa (Viṣṇu) as the supreme reality and teaches the aṣṭākṣara mantra (Oṃ Namo Nārāyaṇāya); its text is represented here as a catalogue entry.
