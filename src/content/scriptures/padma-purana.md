The Padma Purāṇa, one of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas, among the largest at ~55,000 verses in six khaṇḍas (Sṛṣṭi, Bhūmi, Svarga, Pātāla, Uttara, Kriyā-yoga). Organized around the lotus (padma) cosmology. Sṛṣṭi-khaṇḍa: creation, the glory of Puṣkara and other tīrthas; Bhūmi-khaṇḍa: earth geography, rivers, mountains, the Bhārata-varṣa; Svarga-khaṇḍa: heavens, the story of Rāma (Rāmāyaṇa summary), the glory of Ekādaśī; Pātāla-khaṇḍa: netherworlds, the story of Śeṣa, Viṣṇu's yoga-nidrā; Uttara-khaṇḍa: the essence of bhakti, the glory of Viṣṇu's names (Viṣṇu-sahasranāma context), pilgrimage to Brahmāvarta, Kāśī, Prayāga; Kriyā-yoga-khaṇḍa: temple rituals, vrata, dāna, the Puruṣottama-māhātmya (Jagannātha). Major source for tīrtha-māhātmya and Vaiṣṇava ritual.

One of the largest Mahāpurāṇas, organized around the lotus (padma) cosmology with extensive pilgrimage and ritual sections.
