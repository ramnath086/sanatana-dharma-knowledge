The Devī Bhāgavata Purāṇa (Śrī Devī Bhāgavatam), a major Śākta Mahāpurāṇa in 12 skandhas and ~18,000 verses, modeled on the Bhāgavata Purāṇa's structure but centered on the Goddess. Skandha 1: creation, the Goddess as śakti; Skandha 2-3: the Devī's forms, the battle with Mahiṣāsura; Skandha 4: the Devī-gītā (the Goddess's teaching on jñāna, yoga, bhakti); Skandha 5: the Śākta cosmology, the Śrī-cakra, the nine nityās; Skandha 6: the Kālī-krama, the ten Mahāvidyās; Skandha 7: the Navarātrī vrata, the Caṇḍī-pāṭha procedure; Skandha 8-9: the glories of Śākta pīṭhas (Kāmākhyā, Kālikā, Tārāpīṭha); Skandha 10-11: the Rāma and Kṛṣṇa narratives retold from the Śākta perspective; Skandha 12: liberation through Devī-bhakti. The primary text for the Devī-gītā and Śākta theology, influential in Bengal and Assam.
