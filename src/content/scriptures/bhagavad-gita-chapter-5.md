Gītā Chapter 5 (Karma-sannyāsa-yoga) contrasts the path of renunciation (karma-sannyāsa) with the path of action (karma-yoga) and resolves them in the yoga of the self (ātma-yoga). It presents the doctrine of 'the fruit' (phala) and 'the fruitless' (aphala) actions, the two birds (sakhya) on the branch of the world-tree, and the equality (sama) of mind toward pleasure-pain, heat-cold, and friend-foe as the mark of the liberated (jīvanmukta).
