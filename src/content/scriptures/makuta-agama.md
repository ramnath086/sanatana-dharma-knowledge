The Makuṭa Āgama, one of the twenty-eight principal Śaiva Āgamas, focusing on the crown (makuṭa) of Śiva as the symbol of supreme sovereignty and the coronation rites of the liṅga; its royal and ritual symbolism is represented here as a catalogue entry.
