Bhagavad Gītā Chapter 1 (Āropaṇa, 'the setting') presents the Kuru army arrayed, the despair of the Pandavas, and Arjuna's crisis of dharma when he sees the elders and teachers in arms. The 'Oṅkāra' of Arjuna's lament and the Gītā's inaugural 'dharma-kṣetre' set up the conflict between worldly loyalty and righteous duty that the Gītā's discourse resolves through the teaching of yoga.
