The Viṣṇu Purāṇa, one of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas in six aṃśas (parts) and ~23,000 verses, attributed to Parāśara and narrated to Maitreya. Aṃśa 1: creation (sarga), cosmology, the varṇāśrama system; Aṃśa 2: geography (dvīpas, varṣas), Pātāla, hells, the seven dvīpas; Aṃśa 3: Manus, manvantaras, the solar and lunar dynasties (Sūryavaṃśa, Candravaṃśa); Aṃśa 4: detailed genealogies of kings through the ages; Aṃśa 5: Kṛṣṇa's life (Mathurā, Vṛndāvana, Dvārakā, Jarāsandha, Kālayavana, the Pāṇḍavas); Aṃśa 6: dissolution (pralaya), the path of liberation, the glories of Viṣṇu-bhakti. The earliest and most systematic Vaiṣṇava Purāṇa, foundational for later bhakti traditions.
