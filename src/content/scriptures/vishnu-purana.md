The Viṣṇu Purāṇa, one of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas in six aṃśas, focusing on Viṣṇu's avatāras, cosmology, and the genealogy of the solar and lunar dynasties; its influence on later Vaiṣṇava theology is represented here as a catalogue entry.
