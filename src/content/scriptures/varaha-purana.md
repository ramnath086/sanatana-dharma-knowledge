The Varāha Purāṇa, one of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas in 217 chapters and ~24,000 verses, narrated by Varāha (Viṣṇu's boar avatar) to Bhūdevī. Focuses on tīrthas (pilgrimage sites), vrata (vows), and Viṣṇu-bhakti. Major sections: the rescue of Bhūdevī, the Varāha-kṣetra (Tirupati), the Śrīveṅkaṭeśa-māhātmya, the Badarī-kṣetra, the Puṣkara-māhātmya, the Gayā-śrāddha-vidhi, the Mathurā-māhātmya, the Dvārakā-māhātmya, the Kāśī-māhātmya, the Prayāga-māhātmya, and numerous vrata-kathās (Śrāvaṇa, Kārtika, Mārgaśīrṣa). The most detailed tīrtha-māhātmya text among the Purāṇas for North and Central Indian Vaiṣṇava sites.
