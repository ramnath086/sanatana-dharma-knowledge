The tenth Maṇḍala of the Ṛgveda Saṃhitā contains 191 hymns attributed to various ṛṣis; contains the Puruṣa Sūkta (10.90), Nāsadīya Sūkta (10.129), and hymns on cosmology, philosophy, and social life; represented here as a catalogue entry.
