The Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa, one of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas in 137 chapters and ~9,000 verses, narrated as a dialogue between the sage Mārkaṇḍeya and Jaimini. Chapters 1-42: cosmology, creation, genealogies, the manvantaras; Chapters 43-80: the glory of the Sun (Sūrya), the legend of King Harishchandra; Chapters 81-93: the Devī Māhātmya (Durgā Saptashatī / Caṇḍī-pāṭha) — the Goddess's battles against Madhu-Kaiṭabha, Mahiṣāsura, and Śumbha-Niśumbha, the foundation of Śākta worship and Navarātrī; Chapters 94-137: the story of Dama, the glories of the Goddess, liberation through Devī-bhakti. The primary textual source for the Caṇḍī-pāṭha recited during Durgā Pūjā and Navarātrī across India, especially Bengal.
