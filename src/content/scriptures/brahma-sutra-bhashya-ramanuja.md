Rāmānuja's Śrī Bhāṣya, the principal Viśiṣṭādvaita commentary on the Brahma Sūtra, establishing qualified non-duality with Viṣṇu as the supreme Brahman qualified by conscious souls and matter; its refutation of Advaita and its systematic exposition of viśiṣṭādvaita are represented here as a catalogue entry.
