The Kalpa Sūtras are the Vedāṅga texts of ritual procedure, comprising Śrauta Sūtras (Vedic sacrifices), Gṛhya Sūtras (domestic rites), and Dharma Sūtras (social/legal norms); their textual content is represented here as a catalogue entry.
