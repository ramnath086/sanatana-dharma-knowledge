Mandala VI of the Ṛg Veda, named after the sage Gotama Āraṇya, is the 'Āraṇya-mandala' and the last of the 'family books' (kula-sūktas). It contains the famous 'Hymn of the Two Paths' (dvayi-vāk) of the sage of the two ways, the early formulation of the doctrine of the three worlds, and the hymn to the Asvins. Mandala VI is a source for the earliest Indian medicine (āyurveda) and the first glimpses of the 'worldly' (laukika) concerns of the later Vedic ritual.
