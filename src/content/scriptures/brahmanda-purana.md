The Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa, one of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas in four parts (prakriyā, anuṣaṅga, upodghāta, upasaṃhāra) and ~12,000 verses. Details the cosmic egg (brahmāṇḍa), creation cycles, geography of the seven dvīpas, the Aṣṭādaśa-śakti-pīṭhas (eighteen Goddess shrines), and the Lalitopākhyāna (narrative of Lalitā Tripurasundarī) in the Uttarārdha. Contains the Āditya-hṛdaya, the Navagraha-stotras, and detailed pilgrimage (tīrtha) sections for Kāñcī, Śrīśaila, and other Śākta sites. Closely related to the Vāyu Purāṇa; together they preserve the Āditya and Śaiva-Śākta cosmologies. Key for Śākta tradition and South Indian temple mythology.
