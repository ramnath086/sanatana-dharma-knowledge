Gītā Chapter 12 (Bhakti-yoga, 'the yoga of devotion') is prefaced by Arjuna's question (which of the 'higher' path — of the formless [nirguṇa] or the 'with-form' [saguṇa]). Kṛṣṇa answers that the path of devotion (bhakti) is easier for the embodied, and describes the 'equal-minded' (sama-caraṇa) devotee who sees the self (ātman) and the deity (īśvara) as one and serves all beings as manifestations of the supreme.
