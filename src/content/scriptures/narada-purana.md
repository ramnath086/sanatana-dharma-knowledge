The Nārada Purāṇa, one of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas in two parts (Pūrvabhāga and Uttarabhāga) and ~25,000 verses, attributed to Nārada and narrated to the sages. Pūrvabhāga: the glory of Viṣṇu, the ten avatāras, the Gāyatrī-māhātmya, the Ekādaśī-vrata, the Śrāvaṇa-māsa-māhātmya, pilgrimage to Badarī, Dvārakā, Prayāga, Kāśī, Gayā; Uttarabhāga: the Bhakti-sūtras, the Nārada-bhakti-sūtra context, the Viṣṇu-sahasranāma, the Rāma-nāma-māhātmya, the glories of the Tulasī and Śālagrāma, the rules for Vaiṣṇava conduct (samayācāra). A comprehensive Vaiṣṇava dharma-śāstra and tīrtha-māhātmya text.
