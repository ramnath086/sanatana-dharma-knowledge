The Vāmana Purāṇa, one of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas in 95 chapters, narrated by Pulastya to Nārada, focusing on Viṣṇu's Vāmana avatar, the Bali-Vāmana narrative, and the tīrthas of Kurukṣetra; its role in Vaiṣṇava pilgrimage traditions is represented here as a catalogue entry.
