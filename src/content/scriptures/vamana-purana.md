The Vāmana Purāṇa, one of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas in 95 chapters and ~10,000 verses, narrated by Pulastya to Nārada. Focuses on Viṣṇu's Vāmana avatāra, the Bali-Vāmana narrative (three strides covering the three worlds), and the tīrthas of Kurukṣetra. Also covers: the Dakṣa-yajña and Śiva's destruction of it, the birth of Gaṅgā, the Sarasvatī-māhātmya, the Yamunā-māhātmya, the Gayā-śrāddha, the Prayāga-māhātmya, the Kāśī-māhātmya, and the glories of the Nṛsiṃha avatāra. A key text for Kurukṣetra pilgrimage and the Vāmana-Nṛsiṃha dual focus of Viṣṇu worship.
