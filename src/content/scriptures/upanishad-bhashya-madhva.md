Madhva's commentaries on the ten principal Upaniṣads, presenting the Dvaita interpretation of the mahāvākyas, the eternal distinction between jīva and Brahman, and the role of bhakti; his refutation of Advaita's identity thesis is represented here as a catalogue entry.
