Gītā Chapter 4 (Jñāna-karma-saṅgama) presents the simultaneous path of knowledge and action, the doctrine of the 'divine birth' (daśa-guṇa) of the avatar, and Kṛṣṇa's revelation that he has taught the imperishable yoga to the sun (Vaivasvata) and through the ages to the seers. It is the source of the celebrated line 'sarva-dharmān parityajya māṁ ekam śaraṇaṁ vraja' (abandon all dharmas and take refuge in me alone).
