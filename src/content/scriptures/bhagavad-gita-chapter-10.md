Gītā Chapter 10 (Vibhūti-yoga, 'the manifestation') presents the 'glories' (vibhūti) of the Supreme, enumerating the divine powers (īśvara-bala) and the supreme Person's manifestations as kings, as the Vedas, and as the foremost of the devotees. It balances the transcendence (para) and the immanence (aparā) and affirms that the wise see the same (ekatva) in all beings.
