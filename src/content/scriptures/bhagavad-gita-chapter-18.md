Gītā Chapter 18 (Mokṣa-yoga, 'the yoga of liberation') concludes with the threefold classification of knowledge (jñāna), the doer (karta), and 'renunciation' (tyāga) — 'knowledge-knowledge' (para), 'action-action' (phala), and renunciation (niṣkāma). It summarizes the teaching (the four Puruṣārthas and the three yogas) and Arjuna's final resolution (kṣipra) to fight as the instrument (ṇāyakā) of the divine.
