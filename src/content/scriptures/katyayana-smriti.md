The Kātyāyana Smṛti, a Dharmaśāstra text attributed to Kātyāyana, focusing on vyavahāra, women's property rights (strīdhana), and inheritance law; its influence on later dharma digests (Mitākṣarā, Dāyabhāga) is represented here as a catalogue entry.
