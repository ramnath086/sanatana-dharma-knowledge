Mandala IV of the Ṛg Veda, the 'Agni-mandala,' is dominated by hymns to Agni (the fire-god and the personification of the sacrificial oblation) and to the Maruts (the storm-gods of the atmospheric class). Its hymns describe Agni as the Hotṛ (priest of the sacrifice), the divine messenger who conveys the offerings to the gods, and the witness of oaths (dṛu). The Mandala's structure reflects the centrality of the ritual fire (yajña) in the early Vedic household and the sacral kingship (rājā) it secures.
