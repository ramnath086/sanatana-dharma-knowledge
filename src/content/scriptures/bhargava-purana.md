The Bhārgava Purāṇa, one of the eighteen Upapurāṇas, attributed to the sage Bhṛgu, focusing on dharma, prāyaścitta, and the worship of Viṣṇu; its role in Dharmaśāstra traditions is represented here as a catalogue entry.
