The Skanda Purāṇa, the largest Mahāpurāṇa at ~81,000+ verses in seven khaṇḍas (Māheśvara, Viṣṇu, Brahma, Kāśī, Āvantya, Nāgara, Prabhāsa). Māheśvara-khaṇḍa: Śiva's glory, the twelve jyotirliṅgas, Kedāra-khaṇḍa (Himalayan tīrthas); Viṣṇu-khaṇḍa: Viṣṇu avatāras, Badarī, Puruṣottama (Jagannātha); Brahma-khaṇḍa: creation, Brahmā's life, Puṣkara, Gayā; Kāśī-khaṇḍa: Kāśī/Avimiukta, the glory of Kāśī, the Annapūrṇā temple; Āvantya-khaṇḍa: Avantī/Ujjayinī, Mahākāleśvara, the Siṃhastha Kumbha; Nāgara-khaṇḍa: Gujarat tīrthas, Dvārakā, Prabhāsa; Prabhāsa-khaṇḍa: Somnāth, the Yādava dynasty, the end of Kṛṣṇa's līlā. The definitive tīrtha-māhātmya text for Śaiva and pan-Hindu pilgrimage.
