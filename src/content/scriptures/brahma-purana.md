The Brahma Purāṇa, one of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas in ~10,000 verses, traditionally ascribed to Brahmā and narrated to the sages at Naimiṣāraṇya. Covers creation (sarga), the solar and lunar dynasties, the geography of Bhārata-varṣa, the seven dvīpas, the glory of the Godāvarī and other rivers, the Śiva and Viṣṇu kṣetras (Ekāmra, Purī, Koṇārka, Kāśī), the Purī Jagannātha temple, the Ratha-yātrā, and the Saura and Gāṇapatya traditions. Contains the Gautamī-māhātmya (Godāvarī pilgrimage) and the Koṇārka-māhātmya. Bridges Vaiṣṇava, Śaiva, and Saura traditions; especially important for Odisha and Andhra temple mythology.
