Gītā Chapter 9 (Rāja-yoga, 'the king of knowledge') is the 'jewel of all teachings,' declaring the supreme Person (parama-puruṣa) Kṛṣṇa and the path of devotion (bhakti) that is open to all (the outcaste, the woman, the devotee alike). It is the locus of the 'equal-eyed' (sama-darśi) vision and the teaching that the supreme pervades all beings and yet is distinct from them (anumantavyāḥ).
