The Matsya Purāṇa, one of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas in ~14,000 verses and 291 chapters, narrated by Matsya (Viṣṇu's fish avatar) to Manu. Covers the Matsya avatāra and the flood legend, the seven Manus, royal genealogies (Sūryavaṃśa, Candravaṃśa), the five characteristics of a Purāṇa (pañca-lakṣaṇa), temple architecture (vāstu-śāstra: prāsāda, maṇḍapa, gopura, pratimā-lakṣaṇa), iconography of major deities, vrata rituals (Śivarātri, Caturmāsya, Ekādaśī), the Śrāddha-vidhi, the Prayāga-māhātmya, the Kāśī-māhātmya, and the Narmadā-māhātmya. The earliest Purāṇic source for Hindu temple architecture and iconography.
