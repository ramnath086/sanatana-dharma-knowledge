The Garuḍa Purāṇa, one of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas in ~19,000 verses, narrated by Garuḍa to Kaśyapa (or Viṣṇu to Garuḍa). Two main parts: Pūrvakhanda (Ācāra-kāṇḍa, Dharma-kāṇḍa, Preta-kāṇḍa) and Uttarakhanda (Brahma-kāṇḍa). Covers Viṣṇu worship, saṃskāras, vrata, dāna, temple construction, iconography, gemology (ratna-śāstra), astrology, medicine (Āyurveda), the afterlife journey (pretakalpā), the realms of Yama, funeral rites (antyeṣṭi), śrāddha, and the path to liberation through Viṣṇu-bhakti. Authoritative for death rituals and śrāddha procedures across Hindu traditions.
