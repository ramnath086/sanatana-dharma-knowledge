The Vāyu Purāṇa, a Śaiva Mahāpurāṇa in four padas (prakriyā, anuṣaṅga, upodghāta, upasaṃhāra) and ~24,000 verses, narrated by Vāyu to the sages. Covers creation cycles, the kalpas and manvantaras, the genealogies of kings and sages (sūryavaṃśa, candravaṃśa), the geography of the seven dvīpas and Bhārata-varṣa, the Śiva kṣetras (Kāśī, Gayā, Puṣkara, Śrīśaila, Kedāra), the Aṣṭādaśa-śakti-pīṭhas, the Gayā-māhātmya and the Śrāddha-vidhi. Closely related to the Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa (shares the Lalitopākhyāna and Āditya-hṛdaya). Preserves the early Śaiva cosmology and the genealogical tradition of the Purāṇas.
