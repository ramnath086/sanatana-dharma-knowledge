Gītā Chapter 16 (Daivāsura-yoga, 'divine and demonic') contrasts the divine (daivī) qualities (fear of God, non-violence, truthfulness, self-control) with the demonic (āsurī) (hypocrisy, arrogance, violence, anger). It teaches that the divine-natured inherit the supreme (para) while the demoniac are doomed to repeated birth; it is the explicit moral framework of the Gītā's teaching.
