The sixteenth chapter of the Bhagavad Gītā (Daivāsura Sampad Vibhāga Yoga), contrasting the divine qualities (daivī sampad) with the demonic qualities (āsurī sampad), and their consequences for liberation or bondage; its 24 verses contrast the two paths; represented here as a catalogue entry.
