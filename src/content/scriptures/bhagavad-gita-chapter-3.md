Gītā Chapter 3 (Karma-yoga) establishes action (karma) as inescapable for embodied beings and teaches the yoga of selfless action (niṣkāma karma). It introduces the doctrine of the 'wheel' (cakara) of action that cannot be escaped by the seeker, the teaching that performance must be dedicated to the divine (īśvarārpaṇa), and the example of the sage who acts without attachment (yukta-vairāgya).
