The Gṛhya Sūtras, the Vedāṅga texts of domestic rites (saṃskāras): marriage (vivāha), conception (garbhādhāna), birth (jātakarma), naming (nāmakaraṇa), first feeding (annaprāśana), tonsure (cūḍākaraṇa), initiation (upanayana), marriage, and funeral (antyeṣṭi); their relationship to Śrauta Sūtras and Dharma Sūtras is represented here as a catalogue entry.
