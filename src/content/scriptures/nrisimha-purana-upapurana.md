The Nṛsiṃha Upapurāṇa is one of the eighteen Upapurāṇas, focusing on the Nṛsiṃha (man-lion) avatar of Viṣṇu. It contains stories of the devotee Prahlāda, the defeat of the demon Hiraṇyakaipu, and the symbolism of the Nṛsiṃha form as the Supreme Being who transcends all categories. The text also includes a description of the iconography, the sacred places associated with Nṛsiṃha worship, and the theological significance of the avatar. It is associated with the Vaiṣṇava tradition and is recited during the monthly Nṛsiṃha-vātaṇa and the annual Nṛsiṃha-jayantī festival.
