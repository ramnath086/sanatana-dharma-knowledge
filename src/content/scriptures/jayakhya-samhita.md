The Jayākhya Saṃhitā, a major Vaiṣṇava Pāñcarātra text in 33 chapters, expounding the five forms of Viṣṇu (para, vyūha, vibhava, antaryāmin, arcā), the fivefold worship (pañcakāla), and the philosophy of Viṣṇu as supreme; represented here as a catalogue entry.
