The Liṅga Purāṇa, one of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas in ~11,000 verses and two parts (Pūrvabhāga and Uttarabhāga). Pūrvabhāga: the origin of the liṅga (liṅgodbhava), the glory of the liṅga, the twenty-eight Śaiva Āgamas, the five faces of Śiva (Sadyojāta, Vāmadeva, Aghora, Tatpuruṣa, Īśāna), the eight forms (aṣṭamūrti), the twelve jyotirliṅgas; Uttarabhāga: the Liṅga-gītā (Śiva's teaching to the sages), the story of Dhruva, the Puṣkara-māhātmya, the glory of Kāśī, the Śiva-rātra vrata, the rites of liṅga-pratiṣṭhā and daily worship (nitya-pūjā). The definitive textual authority for liṅga worship in Śaiva temple tradition.
