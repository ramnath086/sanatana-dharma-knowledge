The Liṅga Purāṇa, one of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas, focusing on the worship of Śiva in the form of the liṅga, the origin of the liṅga, and the glory of Śiva's various forms; its role in Śaiva ritual and temple worship is represented here as a catalogue entry.
