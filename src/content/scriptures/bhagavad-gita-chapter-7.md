Gītā Chapter 7 (Jñāna-vijñāna-yoga) declares Kṛṣṇa as the supreme (para) of the knowledge and the secret of his being (guhyatama), and teaches the four kinds of seekers (the distressed, the desirous, the seeker of knowledge, and the learned). It is the doctrinal basis of the Kṛṣṇa-centric bhakti of the Gauḍīya and presents the 'knowledge-that-is-secret-of-the-self' (jñāna) as the key to the whole teaching.
