The seventh chapter of the Bhagavad Gītā (Jñāna Vijñāna Yoga), revealing the nature of the divine (Bhagavān) as both the material and efficient cause of the universe, and the distinction between lower (aparā) and higher (parā) knowledge; its 30 verses reveal the nature of the supreme; represented here as a catalogue entry.
