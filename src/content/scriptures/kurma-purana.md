The Kūrma Purāṇa, one of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas in two parts (Pūrvabhāga and Uttarabhāga) and ~17,000 verses, narrated by Kūrma (Viṣṇu's tortoise avatar) to the sages at Naimiṣāraṇya. Pūrvabhāga: creation, the four puruṣārthas (dharma, artha, kāma, mokṣa), the glory of the Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa-hṛdaya, the unity of Śiva and Viṣṇu, the ṛṣi genealogies; Uttarabhāga: the Īśvara-gītā (Śiva's teaching on yoga and jñāna), the Vyāsa-gītā, the Śiva-gītā, pilgrimage to Kāśī, Prayāga, Gayā, and the Śiva kṣetras. A synthesis text emphasizing Śiva-Viṣṇu non-difference (abheda) and the yoga of knowledge and devotion.
