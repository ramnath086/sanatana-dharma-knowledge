The Kālikā Purāṇa, one of the eighteen Upapurāṇas, a Śākta text focusing on the worship of Kālī, the ten Mahāvidyās, and the ritual practices of Śākta traditions in Kamarupa (Assam); its tantric orientation is represented here as a catalogue entry.
