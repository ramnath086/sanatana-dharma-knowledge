Mandala II of the Ṛg Veda centres on the Āditya family: Mitra, Varuṇa, Aryaman, and the twin deities (Mitra-twins) of cosmic order (ṛta). Its hymns (especially the Varuṇa and Mitra sūktas) articulate the moral and cosmic dimensions of the Vedic conception of law, the guest-sacrament (atithya), and the early 'contract' theology (mitāṅkatā). The mandala preserves the oldest formulation of the 'two paths' (dvayi-vāk) and the earliest allusions to the sacrifice (yajña) as the cosmogonic act.
