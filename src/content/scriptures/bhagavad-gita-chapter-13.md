The thirteenth chapter of the Bhagavad Gītā (Kṣetra Kṣetrajña Vibhāga Yoga), distinguishing the field (kṣetra, the body) from the knower of the field (kṣetrajña, the self), and describing the nature of both; its 35 verses analyze the relationship between body and self; represented here as a catalogue entry.
