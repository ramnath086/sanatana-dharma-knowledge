Gītā Chapter 13 (Kṣetra-kṣetrātmārāmāya, 'the field and the knower of the field') presents the doctrine of the field (kṣetra, the body-mind) and the knower (kṣetrajña, the self) as the subject of discriminative knowledge (jñāna). It identifies the supreme knowledge (parama-jñāna) with the knowledge of the field-kṣetraṅkṣaṇa (the self's distinction from prakṛti) and teaches that such knowledge brings the seeker to the supreme (para).
