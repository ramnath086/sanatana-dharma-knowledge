The Viṣṇu Dharmottara Purāṇa, a major Upapurāṇa supplementing the Viṣṇu Purāṇa, covering temple architecture (vāstu), iconography (pratimā-lakṣaṇa), painting (citra), dance (nṛtya), and the Viṣṇu bhakti tradition; its role in temple arts is represented here as a catalogue entry.
