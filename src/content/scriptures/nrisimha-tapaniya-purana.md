The Nṛsiṃha Tāpanīya Purāṇa, one of the eighteen Upapurāṇas, a Vaiṣṇava text focusing on the Nṛsiṃha avatar, the Nṛsiṃha mantra, and the meditation on the fierce form of Viṣṇu; its role in Nṛsiṃha worship is represented here as a catalogue entry.
