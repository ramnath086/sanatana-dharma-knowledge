The eighth chapter of the Bhagavad Gītā (Akṣara Brahma Yoga), describing the imperishable Brahman, the practice of remembering the divine at the time of death, and the two paths of departure (arcirādi and dhūmādi); its 28 verses reveal the path to the supreme; represented here as a catalogue entry.
