Gītā Chapter 8 (Atharva-vākya-yoga, 'the last word') returns to the question of what is the imperishable (akṣara) and what is the supreme (para). It introduces the 'word/verse' (śabda/ākṣara) as the means, presents the death-bed teaching (para-kāla) and the various paths to the supreme goal (including the remembering of the name [nāma-smṛti] and the yoga of the breath).
