The Bhāgavata Purāṇa (Śrīmad Bhāgavatam), one of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas in twelve skandhas, focusing on Kṛṣṇa-bhakti, the daśāvatāra, and the dialogue between Parīkṣit and Śuka; its theological emphasis on bhakti-yoga is represented here as a catalogue entry.
