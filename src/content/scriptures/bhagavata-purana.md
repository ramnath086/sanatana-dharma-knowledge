The Bhāgavata Purāṇa (Śrīmad Bhāgavatam), one of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas in twelve skandhas (books) and ~18,000 verses. Composed as a dialogue between Śuka and King Parīkṣit on the banks of the Gaṅgā. Skandha 1-2: creation, cosmology, and the avatāras; Skandha 3-4: Kapila's Sāṅkhya, Dhruva, Prahlāda; Skandha 5: Bharata, Ṛṣabha, cosmic geography; Skandha 6: Ajāmila, Vaivasvata, Nṛsiṃha; Skandha 7: Prahlāda, Nṛsiṃha; Skandha 8: Vāmana, churning of the ocean; Skandha 9: genealogies; Skandha 10: Kṛṣṇa's līlā (Vṛndāvana, Mathurā, Dvārakā); Skandha 11: Uddhava-gītā, Yadu dynasty; Skandha 12: future, Kali age, Māhātmya. Central to Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism and bhakti traditions across India.
