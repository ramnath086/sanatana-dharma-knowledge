Śaṅkara's commentaries on the ten principal Upaniṣads (Īśa, Kena, Kaṭha, Praśna, Muṇḍaka, Māṇḍūkya, Taittirīya, Aitareya, Chāndogya, Bṛhadāraṇyaka); his Advaita interpretation of mahāvākyas, the nature of ātman/Brahman, and the refutation of duality are represented here as a catalogue entry.
