Gītā Chapter 11 (Vilāpa-yoga, 'the universal form') records Arjuna's petition to see the supreme Person's cosmic form (viśvarūpa), which Kṛṣṇa grants. The vision is cosmic (the whole of time and space) and terrifying (kālī), revealing the universal (sarvataḥ pāṇi) yet intimate presence of the divine. Arjuna's grief and surrender (praṇāma) mark the culmination of his bhakti and the 'crowning' (śiras) of the Gītā's teaching.
