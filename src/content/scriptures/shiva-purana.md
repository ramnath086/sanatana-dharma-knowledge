The Śiva Purāṇa, one of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas in seven saṃhitās (Vidyeśvara, Rudra, Śatarudra, Koṭirudra, Umā, Kailāsa, Vāyavīya) and ~24,000 verses. Saṃhitā 1 (Vidyeśvara): glory of Śiva, liṅga worship, jyotirliṅgas; Saṃhitā 2 (Rudra): Rudra's forms, the eleven Rudras; Saṃhitā 3 (Śatarudra): hundred names of Rudra, Śiva's cosmic dance; Saṃhitā 4 (Koṭirudra): twelve jyotirliṅgas (Somnath, Mallikārjuna, Mahākāleśvara, Omkāreśvara, Kedārnāth, Bhīmaśaṅkar, Viśvanāth, Tryambakeśvara, Vaidyanāth, Nāgeśvara, Rāmeśvara, Ghṛṣṇeśvara); Saṃhitā 5 (Umā): Pārvatī's birth, marriage, Gaṇeśa, Skanda; Saṃhitā 6 (Kailāsa): Kailāsa, the yoga of Śiva, the five faces; Saṃhitā 7 (Vāyavīya): Vāyu's narration, the Śiva-gītā, liberation through Śiva-bhakti. Central to Śaiva Siddhānta and temple traditions.
