Valmiki (or Valmika) is the traditional adikavi ('first poet') of Sanskrit literature and the ṛṣi attributed with the Rāmāyaṇa. The Vālmiki-Rāmāyaṇa (c. 24,000 ślokas) is the earliest Sanskrit epic and the source for all later Rāmāyaṇas. The Śāṇḍikas and later tradition recount his transformation from a dacoit (māṃsa) into a sage by the grace of Nārada and the Gāyatrī-mantra, making him the patron of the kāvya genre and the archetypal compiler (vyāsa) of narrative (kathā) poetry in Sanskrit.
