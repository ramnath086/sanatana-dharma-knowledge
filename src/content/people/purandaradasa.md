Purandaradāsa (1484–1564), the 'father of Carnatic music' (Karnataka Sangeeta Pitamaha), composer of 475,000+ kīrtanas in Kannada and Sanskrit; his systematization of music pedagogy (sarali, janta, alankara, geetam, swarajati, varnam), his devotion to Viṭṭhala, and his role in the Haridāsa movement are represented here as a catalogue entry.
