Swami Vivekananda (1863–1902), the chief disciple of Rāmakṛṣṇa, the founder of the Ramakrishna Mission, the exponent of practical Vedānta at the 1893 World's Parliament of Religions (Chicago); his synthesis of Advaita with social service, his call for 'man-making education', and his role in modern Hindu renaissance are represented here as a catalogue entry.
