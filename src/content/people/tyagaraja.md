Tyāgarāja (1767–1847), one of the Carnatic trinity, composer of ~24,000 kṛtis in Telugu and Sanskrit, mostly on Rāma; his pancharatna kṛtis, his devotion to Rāma, his life in Tiruvaiyaru, and his influence on Carnatic music pedagogy are represented here as a catalogue entry.
