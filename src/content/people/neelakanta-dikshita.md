Nīlakaṇṭha Dīkṣita (1613–1697), the Advaita Vedānta scholar and poet, grandson of Appayya Dīkṣita; author of the Śiva-leśa, Śivalīlārṇava, and the satirical Kaliviḍambana; his role in the Śrīvidyā and Advaita traditions in South India is represented here as a catalogue entry.
