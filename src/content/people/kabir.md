Kabīr (c. 1440–1518), the mystic poet-saint of the Bhakti movement, synthesizing Hindu and Islamic devotional traditions; his verses (bījak, sākhīs, śabdas) emphasizing nirguṇa-bhakti (devotion to the formless), the guru, and the critique of ritual orthodoxy; his influence on the Kabīr Panth and Sikh scripture (Gurū Granth Sāhib) is represented here as a catalogue entry.
