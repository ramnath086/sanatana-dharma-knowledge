Tulsīdās (1532–1623), the composer of the Rāmacaritamānasa (Awadhi retelling of the Rāmāyaṇa), the Hanumān Cālīsā, and the Vinaya Patrikā; his role in popularizing Rāma-bhakti in North India, his founding of the Sankat Mochan Temple (Varanasi), and his influence on the Rāmānandī tradition are represented here as a catalogue entry.
