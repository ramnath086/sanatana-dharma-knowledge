Rāmakṛṣṇa Paramahaṃsa (1836–1886), the 19th-century mystic of Dakṣiṇeśvar, devotee of Kālī, who experienced diverse religious paths (Śākta, Vaiṣṇava, Advaita, Islam, Christianity) and declared 'yato mat tato patha' (as many paths, so many ways); his disciple Vivekananda, the Ramakrishna Mission, and his universalist message are represented here as a catalogue entry.
