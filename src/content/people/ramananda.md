Rāmānanda (c. 14th–15th century), the founder of the Rāmānandī sect, a North Indian Vaiṣṇava tradition emphasizing Rāma-bhakti and the accessibility of devotion regardless of caste; his disciples included Kabīr, Ravidās, and Sena; his role in the Bhakti movement in North India is represented here as a catalogue entry.
