Muttusvāmi Dīkṣitar (1775–1835), one of the Carnatic trinity, composer of ~500 kṛtis in Sanskrit, known for his mastery of rāga, the navagraha kṛtis, the kamalamba navavarṇa kṛtis, and his use of Sanskrit sahitya with intricate rāga structures; his influence on Carnatic music theory is represented here as a catalogue entry.
