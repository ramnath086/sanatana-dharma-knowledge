Śyāma Śāstri (1762–1827), one of the Carnatic trinity, composer of ~300 kṛtis in Telugu and Sanskrit, known for his swarajatis, his kamakshi navaratna mālikā, his ananda bhairavi compositions, and his distinctive rhythmic structures (tāḷa); his influence on Carnatic music is represented here as a catalogue entry.
