Kanakadāsa (1509–1609), the Haridāsa saint-poet from Karnataka, composer of kīrtanas in Kannada dedicated to Adikeshava (Viṣṇu); his works (Mohanatarangini, Ramadhanya Charitre, Nalacharitre) emphasizing bhakti, social equality, and devotion over caste; his association with Udupi and the Krishna temple are represented here as a catalogue entry.
