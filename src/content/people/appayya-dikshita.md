Appayya Dīkṣita (1520–1593), the Advaita Vedānta scholar and Śaiva devotee, author of over 100 works including the Siddhānta-leśa-saṅgraha, Śivārkamaṇi-dīpikā, and Khaṇḍana-khaṇḍa-khādya; his synthesis of Advaita and Śaiva Siddhānta, his debate with Vijayeन्द्र Tīrtha, and his role in the Śrīvidyā tradition are represented here as a catalogue entry.
