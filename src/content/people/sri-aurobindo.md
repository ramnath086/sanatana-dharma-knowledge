Śrī Aurobindo (1872–1950), the nationalist, philosopher, and yogi who developed Integral Yoga (Pūrṇa Yoga); his works (The Life Divine, Synthesis of Yoga, Savitri), his concept of the Supermind, the descent of consciousness, the Ashram at Pondicherry, and the Auroville township are represented here as a catalogue entry.
