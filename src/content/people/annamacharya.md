Annamācārya (1408–1503), the earliest known composer of saṅkīrtana (devotional songs) in Telugu, dedicated to Veṅkaṭeśvara of Tirumala; his 32,000+ compositions (saṅkīrtanas) on copper plates recovered in the 20th century, his role in the Śrīvaiṣṇava tradition, and his influence on Carnatic music are represented here as a catalogue entry.
