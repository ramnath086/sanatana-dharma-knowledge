The Tirumanikūṇḍa Temple at Kanyakumari district (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Tirumanikūṇḍa Perumāl. The annual festival is celebrated during the Tamil month of Aippāsi. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple is associated with the legend of the precious stone (mani) and the divine intervention.
