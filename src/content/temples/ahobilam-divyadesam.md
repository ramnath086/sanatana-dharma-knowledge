The Nava Narasiṃha Temple complex at Ahobilam (Nandyal district, Andhra Pradesh), a Divya Deśam with nine shrines for the nine forms of Narasiṃha (Nava Narasiṃha): Ahobila, Bhārgava, Yogananda, Chatravata, Krodha, Malola, Jvālā, Pavana, and Karanja. Spread across the Nallamala Hills. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār and Nammāḻvār. The annual Brahmotsavam and the Narasiṃha-jayantī attract thousands. Unique for the nine distinct Narasiṃha forms in one pilgrimage circuit.
