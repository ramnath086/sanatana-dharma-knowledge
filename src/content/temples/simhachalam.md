The Varāha Lakṣmī Narasiṃha Temple at Simhachalam (Simhādri), Andhra Pradesh, dedicated to Narasiṃha as the 'lion-hill' deity; its Kalinga-Chola architecture, the sandalwood paste covering (candana), the annual Candanotsava festival, and its importance in the Śrīvaiṣṇava tradition are represented here as a catalogue entry.
