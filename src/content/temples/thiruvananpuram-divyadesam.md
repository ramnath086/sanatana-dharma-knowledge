The Anantasayana Temple at Tiruvānana-pura (Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Anantasayana (reclining on the cosmic serpent). The deity is in reclining posture on Ādiśeṣa, with the hill temple offering a panoramic view. The annual Anavasyava Ekādaśī and the annual temple car festival are celebrated. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple gives its name to the city of Thiruvananthapuram.
