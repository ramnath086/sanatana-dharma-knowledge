The Ahobilam Temple complex in Andhra Pradesh, the principal shrine of Narasiṃha (Viṣṇu's man-lion avatar), with nine temples (Nava-Narasiṃha) on the Nallamala hills; its association with the Prahlāda narrative, the Śrīvaiṣṇava tradition, and the Ahobila Maṭha are represented here as a catalogue entry.
