The Sāraṅgāpani Temple at Kumbakonam (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Sāraṅgāpani (holding the bow Sāraṅga). The 12th-century Cōḷa temple with a 50m tall gopura and the unique chariot-shaped (ratha) sanctum. The chariot festival (Rathotsava) is famous. Glorified by Nammāḻvār, Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār, Poygai Āḻvār, and Periyalvār. The temple tank (Hema-puṣkariṇī) is sacred. One of the five Pañcarātra temples (with Śrīraṅgam, Tirupati, Kāñcī, and Badrinath).
