The Tirukkoṣṭiyūr Temple (Sowrīrāja Perumāl) in Sivaganga district (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam where Viṣṇu taught the Tirumantra (Oṃ Namo Nārāyaṇāya) to Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The deity is Sowrīrāja Perumāl with Śrīdevi and Bhūdevi. The temple is the birthplace of Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār's guru (Nampiḷḷai). Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār and Nammāḻvār. The annual Tirumantra-upadeśa festival commemorates the teaching.
