The Varadarāja Perumal Temple (Devarāja-svāmī) at Kāñcīpuram (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Varadarāja (the King who grants boons). The 11th-century Cōḷa structure with a 100-pillar mandapa and the Athi Varadar (the original wooden deity) preserved in the temple tank. Glorified by Nammāḻvār, Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār, Poygai Āḻvār, and Bhūta Āḻvār. One of the Kāñcī Divya Deśams along with Yathothkāri, Ulagalantha Perumal, and others. The Garuḍa-sevā festival is famous.
