The Varāha Narasiṃha Temple at Simhācalam (Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Varāha Narasiṃha (the boar-man-lion form). The deity is covered in sandalwood paste year-round (revealed only on Akṣaya Tritīyā). The Kalinga-style architecture with a 96-pillar mandapa. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The annual Candana-yātrā (sandalwood procession) and the Narasiṃha-jayantī are major festivals. One of the few Divya Deśams with a Narasiṃha primary deity.
