The Oppiliappan Temple (Uppiliappan) near Kumbakonam (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Oppiliappan (the Lord who accepts even saltless offerings). The legend: Lakṣmī as Bhūmidevi offered food without salt; Viṣṇu accepted it, hence "Uppu-illa-appan" (Lord without salt). No salt is used in the temple kitchen. Glorified by Nammāḻvār and Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple tank (Tula-puṣkariṇī) and the annual Pavitrotsavam are notable.
