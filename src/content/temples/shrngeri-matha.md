The Śṛṅgerī Śāradā Pīṭham (Śṛṅgerī Maṭha) in Karnataka, the first of the four Ādi Śaṅkara maṭhas, dedicated to Śāradā (Sarasvatī); its unbroken guru-paramparā, the Vidyāśaṅkara Temple, the Śāradā Temple, and its role in Advaita Vedānta preservation are represented here as a catalogue entry.
