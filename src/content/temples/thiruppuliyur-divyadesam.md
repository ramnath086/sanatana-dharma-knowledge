The Tiruppuliyūr Temple at Cuddalore district (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Tiruppuliyūr Perumāl. Located near the Pulicat Lake. The annual festival is celebrated during the Tamil month of Aippāsi. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple is associated with the story of the tiger (puli) and the devotee.
