The Badrinath Temple (Badarīnātha), a Divya Deśam in the Garhwal Himalayas (Uttarakhand) at 3,133 m, dedicated to Viṣṇu as Badarīnārāyaṇa. One of the four Char Dham pilgrimage sites and one of the 108 Divya Deśams glorified by the Āḻvārs (Nammāḻvār, Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār). The temple houses a 1m black stone image of Viṣṇu in padmāsana. Open only six months a year (Apr-Nov); the idol is moved to Joshimath in winter. Mentioned in the Nālāyira Divya Prabandham.
