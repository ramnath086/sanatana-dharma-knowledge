The Tryambakeśvara Temple at Nashik (Maharashtra), one of the twelve Jyotirliṅgas, situated near the source of the Godāvarī river, with the unique three-faced liṅga representing Brahmā, Viṣṇu, and Śiva; its architecture, the Kumbha Mela, and the Godāvarī puṣkara are represented here as a catalogue entry.
