The Dvārakādhīśa Temple (Dvārakā), a Divya Deśam on the Gujarat coast, dedicated to Viṣṇu as Dvārakādhīśa (Lord of Dvārakā). One of the four Char Dham and one of the 108 Divya Deśams. The original temple is attributed to Vajranābha (Kṛṣṇa's great-grandson); the current structure dates to 15th-16th century. The five-storey śikhara rises 78m. Glorified by Nammāḻvār and Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār in the Nālāyira Divya Prabandham. The Kṛṣṇa janmabhūmi and the nearby Bet Dwarka (Śāṇṭipura) are associated sites.
