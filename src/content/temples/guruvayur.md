The Guruvāyūr Temple (ഗുരുവായൂര്‍ / குருவாயூர்) in Thrissur district, Kerala — "Bhūloka Vaikuṇṭha" (Vaikuṇṭha on Earth). Dedicated to Kṛṣṇa as Guruvāyūrapurṇan (the Lord of Guruvāyūr), a four-armed standing Viṣṇu with conch, discus, mace, lotus. The idol is said to be worshipped by Vāyu and Bṛhaspati (hence Guru-vāyūr). Kerala architecture: copper-plated roof, golden flagstaff (dvaja-stambha), the Chuttambalam (circumambulatory). Famous for elephants (Punnathur Kotta — elephant sanctuary), the daily uṣa-pūjā (3:30 AM), and the Guruvāyūr Śrī Kṛṣṇa Suprabhātam. The annual Ekādaśī (Vṛścika Ekādaśī, Nov-Dec) and the Utsavam (festival, Feb-Mar) with the Āna-yoṭṭam (elephant race) draw millions. The temple's unique appam-prasādam (rice cake) and the Guruvāyūr Devaswom management.

The Guruvāyūr Temple in Kerala, a major Kṛṣṇa pilgrimage site with distinctive Kerala architecture and elephant traditions.
