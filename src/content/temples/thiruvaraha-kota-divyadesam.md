The Tiruvarāha Koṭṭa Temple at Tirupati district (Andhra Pradesh), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Vārāha (boar avatāra). Located near the Tirumala hills. The temple is associated with the rescue of Bhūmi-devī (the Earth goddess) by Vārāha. The annual Vāsudeva Ekādaśī is the main festival. Glorified by Tiruppaṇ Āḻvār, Periyāḻvār, and Tirumāldevar. The nearby cave of Bāṇa is associated with the story. The sanctum houses a 12-foot Vārāha deity.
