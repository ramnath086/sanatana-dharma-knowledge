The Kāmeshvara Temple at Tirukkovilur (Tiruvannamalai district, Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Aparājiteśvara/Kāmeśvara. The Pallava-Chola temple with a 7th-century origin. The annual Maha Śivarātri and the Brahmotsavam are major festivals. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The nearby Tiruvōndrēśvara (Tiruvannamalai) temple of Śiva is part of the same complex.
