The Navamukunda Temple at Tirunāvāya (Malappuram, Kerala), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Navamukunda (nine-faced). On the banks of the Bhāratappuzha (Nīlā) river. The annual Karkkaṭaka-vāvu (ancestor rites) draws thousands. The temple is associated with the Māmāṅkam festival (medieval trade fair). Glorified by Nammāḻvār and Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The Kerala-style architecture with a round sanctum (vṛtta-śikhara). One of the few Divya Deśams in Kerala.
