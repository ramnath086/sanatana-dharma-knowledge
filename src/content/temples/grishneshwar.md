The Gṛhṇeśvara Temple near Ellora (Maharashtra), the twelfth of the twelve Jyotirliṅgas, located near the Ellora Caves (UNESCO World Heritage site); its architecture, the nearby Kailash Temple, and the Shivalay (Śivalaya) tank are represented here as a catalogue entry.
