The Pārthasārathi Temple at Tiruvallikēṇi (Triplicane, Chennai, Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Pārthasārathi (Kṛṣṇa as Arjuna's charioteer). The 8th-century Pallava temple, expanded by the Cōḷas and Vijayanagara. Five sancta: Pārthasārathi, Ranganātha, Rāma, Narasiṃha, and Varāha. The temple tank (Kairavinī) is famous for the float festival (Teppa-utsavam). Glorified by Poygai Āḻvār, Bhūta Āḻvār, Pēy Āḻvār, Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār, and Nammāḻvār. One of the oldest Divya Deśams in an urban setting.
