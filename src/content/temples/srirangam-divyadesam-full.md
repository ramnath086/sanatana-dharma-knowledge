The Śrī Raṅganātha Temple at Śrīraṅgam (Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu), the foremost Divya Deśam and the largest functioning Hindu temple complex (156 acres). Dedicated to Viṣṇu as Raṅganātha (reclining on Ādiśeṣa). Seven concentric prākāras, 21 gopuras (the Rājagopuram is 73m, Asia's tallest). The Āḻvār tradition: all twelve Āḻvārs glorified it; Nammāḻvār's Tiruvāymoḻi was first recited here. The annual Vaikuṇṭha Ekādaśī (21-day festival) draws millions. The temple houses the Āḻvār sannidhis and the Rāmānuja's sanctum.
