The Tirupparavasaram Temple at Tirunelveli district (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Paravasara Perumāl. The annual festival is celebrated during the Tamil month of Aippāsi. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple is associated with the legend of the sage Paravasara and the divine intervention.
