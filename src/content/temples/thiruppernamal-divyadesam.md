The Tiruppernamal Temple (Thiruppernamal) near Tiruvannamalai, Tamil Nadu, a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Tiruppernamal Perumāl. Associated with the story of Pṛthu (the first king to perform yajña) and the giving-up of the kingdom. The annual Aṅga-dwādashi festival is celebrated. Glorified by Nammāḻvār and Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple is also associated with the sage Durvāsa and the Swayambhū (self-manifested) deity.
