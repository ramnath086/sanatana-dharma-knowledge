The Kailāsa Giri Temple at Kailāsa Giri hills (Chittoor district, Andhra Pradesh), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Nārāyaṇa in a reclining posture (aśṭāṅgābhiṣeka form). The temple is built into the hillside. The annual Brahmotsavam and the Nārāyaṇa Ekādaśī are celebrated. Glorified by Tiruppaṇ Āḻvār. The temple offers panoramic views of the surrounding hills and plains.
