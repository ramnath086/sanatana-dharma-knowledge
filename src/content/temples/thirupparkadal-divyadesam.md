The Tiruppāṟkaṭal Temple (the Ocean of Milk) at Kāñcīpuram (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam where Viṣṇu reclines on Ādiśeṣa in the Kṣīrasāgara (ocean of milk). The temple is associated with the Pāṟkaṭal (the milk ocean) where the Devas and Asuras churned for amṛta. Glorified by Poygai Āḻvār, Bhūta Āḻvār, and Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The annual Kṣīrābdhi-navamī festival celebrates the churning. A small but theologically important Divya Deśam representing the cosmic ocean.
