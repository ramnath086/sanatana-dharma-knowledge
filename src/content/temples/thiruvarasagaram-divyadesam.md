The Tiruvarasagara Temple at Guntur district (Andhra Pradesh), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Varasagara Perumāl. The temple is associated with the legend of the sage Agastya and the divine tank. The annual festival is celebrated during the Tamil month of Aippāsi. Glorified by Tiruppaṇ Āḻvār. The temple architecture follows the local style with a modest gopuram.
