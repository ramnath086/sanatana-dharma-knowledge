Śrīraṅgam as the first and foremost of the 108 Divya Deśams (Vaiṣṇava holy sites), the abode of Raṅganātha, praised by all twelve Āḻvārs; its seven enclosures (prākāras), the Raṅganātha shrine, and the Āḻvār-maṅgala-mūla are represented here as a catalogue entry.
