Tirumala as the second of the 108 Divya Deśams, the abode of Veṅkaṭeśvara, praised by nine Āḻvārs; the Ananda Nilayam vimana, the golden gopuram, the laddu prasadam, and the Brahmotsavam are represented here as a catalogue entry.
