The Jagannātha Temple at Purī (Odisha), a Divya Deśam and one of the four Char Dham, dedicated to Viṣṇu as Jagannātha (Lord of the Universe) with Balabhadra and Subhadrā. The iconic wooden deities are renewed every 12-19 years (Nabakalebara). The massive Ratha-yātrā festival draws millions. Glorified by all twelve Āḻvārs in the Nālāyira Divya Prabandham. The temple kitchen (Mahāprasāda) serves 10,000+ daily. The Govardhana-maṭha of Śaṅkara is located here.
