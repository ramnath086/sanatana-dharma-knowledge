The Tiru-Vellāṅkaṇṇi Temple (Vellankanni) at Tiru-Vellāṅkaṇṇi (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Tiru-Vellāṅkaṇṇi Perumāl. The temple is associated with the legend of the sage Agastya and the goddess. The annual festival is celebrated during the Tamil month of Aippāsi. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple is also known as the "Rome of India" for the basilica church built later by Portuguese missionaries.
