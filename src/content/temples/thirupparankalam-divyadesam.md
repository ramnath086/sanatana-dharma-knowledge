The Tirupparāṅkaḷam Temple at Thiruvananthapuram district (Kerala/Tamil Nadu border), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Parāṅkaḷam Perumāl. The annual festival is celebrated during the Tamil month of Aippāsi. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple is associated with the legend of the sage Para and the divine intervention.
