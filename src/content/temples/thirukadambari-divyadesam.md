The Tirukāmākṣaṇa Temple (Thirukadambari) at Kumbakonam (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Tirukāmākṣaṇa Perumāl. The temple name derives from "Tirukāmā" (beloved Lord) and "aṣa" (to praise). The annual Brahmotsavam and the Aṣṭa-dala-pūjā are celebrated. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple is known for its ancient architecture and the annual float festival (Theppam).
