The Tiruppaṭṭi-puliyūr Temple near Cuddalore (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Paṭṭi-puliyūr Perumāl. The annual festival is celebrated during the Tamil month of Aippāsi. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple is associated with the legend of the tiger (puli) and the divine intervention.
