The Tirucitrakoodal Temple (Citra-kūṭal) at Tiruninravur (near Chennai, Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Viṣṇu (Citra-kūṭa Perumāl). The Cōḷa-period temple with beautiful bronze icons. The temple tank (Citra-puṣkariṇī) is associated with the legend of Citragupta. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The annual Brahmotsavam and the Citragupta-pūjā are celebrated. A smaller but historically significant Divya Deśam.
