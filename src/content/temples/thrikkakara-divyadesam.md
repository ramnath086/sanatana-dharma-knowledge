The Tṛkkākkāra Temple (Vāmana Mūrti) at Tṛkkākkāra near Kochi (Kerala), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Vāmana (the dwarf avatāra). The only Divya Deśam in Kerala proper. The legend: Vāmana placed his third step on Mahābali's head here. The annual Onam festival (ten days) celebrates Mahābali's visit, culminating in the Vāmana-jayantī. Glorified by Nammāḻvār and Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple follows the Kerala architectural style with a copper-plated roof.
