The Purī Govardhana Maṭha in Odisha, the fourth of the four Ādi Śaṅkara maṭhas, dedicated to Jagannātha; its location near the Jagannātha Temple, its guru-paramparā, and its role in preserving Advaita Vedānta in Eastern India are represented here as a catalogue entry.
