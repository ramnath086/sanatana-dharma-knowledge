The Tirukuttaḷapādi Temple at Kutralam (Tirunelveli district, Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Kutralan/Kūṭṭalai Perumāl. Located on the banks of the Tambraparni River near the famous Kutralam waterfalls. The annual Brahmotsavam and the Kudamuzhala festival are celebrated. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple is associated with the sage Agastya and the Kutralam region (the "land of the bow" from Sanskrit kodaṁ-lam).
