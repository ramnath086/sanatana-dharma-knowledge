The Vanamāmalai (Tōṭādṛ) Temple at Naṅguṇeri (Tirunelveli district, Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Vaṉamāmalai (the Lord of the forest garland). The headquarters of the Vaṉamāmalai Maṭha (a Śrīvaiṣṇava monastery). The 15th-century temple with a 100-pillar mandapa and beautiful bronzes. Glorified by Nammāḻvār and Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The annual Brahmotsavam and the Vaṉamāmalai Maṭha's festivals are celebrated. An important center for Śrīvaiṣṇava theology and Sanskrit learning.
