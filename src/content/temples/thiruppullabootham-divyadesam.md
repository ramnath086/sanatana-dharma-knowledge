The Tiruppulla-bhūta Temple at Tirunelveli district (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Pulla-bhūta Perumāl. The annual festival is celebrated during the Tamil month of Aippāsi. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple is associated with the legend of the grass (pulla) and the divine intervention.
