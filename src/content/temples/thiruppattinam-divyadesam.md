The Tiruppattinam Temple in Kanyakumari district (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Perumāl. Located on the southern tip of the Tamil coast, near the sea. The annual Brahmotsavam and the Navaratri festivals are celebrated. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple is associated with the sage Agastya and the southern boundary of the ancient Tamil land (Tamilakam).
