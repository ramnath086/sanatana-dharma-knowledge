The Rāmanāthasvāmī Temple at Rāmeśvaram (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam and one of the four Char Dham, dedicated to Viṣṇu as Rāmanātha (Rāma's Lord) — here Viṣṇu and Śiva are worshipped together. One of the twelve Jyotirliṅgas (Rāmanāthaliṅga). The longest temple corridor in India (1,220m with 1,212 pillars). Glorified by Nammāḻvār, Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār, and Kulasekhara Āḻvār. The sacred tīrthas (22 wells) within the complex. The Sethu (Adam's Bridge) to Śrī Lanka is nearby.
