The Tirumalāgiri Temple in Karimnagar district (Telangana/Andhra Pradesh), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Tirumalāgiri Perumāl. Located on a hilltop with panoramic views. The annual Brahmotsavam and the Maṅgapraśana festival are celebrated. Glorified by Tiruppaṇ Āḻvār and Tirumāldevar. The temple is associated with the story of the sage Gautama and the divine tank (Pushkarinī).
