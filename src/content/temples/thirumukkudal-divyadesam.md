The Tirumukkūḍal Temple at Kumbakonam (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to the three deities in one: Viṣṇu (Nārāyaṇa), Brahmā, and Rudra (Śiva). The unique trinity (tri-devata) temple representing the Trimūrti in one sanctum. The annual Brahmotsavam and the Maha Shivaratri are celebrated. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār and Nammāḻvār. The temple represents the concept of Trimūrti in one form (Tirumukkūḍal = "three-joined").
