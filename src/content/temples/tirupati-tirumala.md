The Tirumala Venkateswara Temple at Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh, one of the most visited pilgrimage sites in the world, dedicated to Veṅkaṭeśvara (Viṣṇu); its Dravidian architecture, the Ananda Nilayam vimana, the Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams (TTD) administration, the laddu prasadam, and the annual Brahmotsavam are represented here as a catalogue entry.
