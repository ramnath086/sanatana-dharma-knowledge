The Kāñcī Kāmakōṭi Pīṭham at Kāñcīpuram, Tamil Nadu, a prominent Śaṅkara maṭha (though its direct Śaṅkara lineage is debated), dedicated to Kāmākṣī; its influential guru-paramparā (including Chandrasekharendra Sarasvati), its role in Advaita Vedānta, and its social service activities are represented here as a catalogue entry.
