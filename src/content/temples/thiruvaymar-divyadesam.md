The Tiruvāy-mara Temple near Chennai (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Tiruvāy-mara Perumāl (the Lord under the banyan tree). Associated with the banyan tree (vāy-tree) where Viṣṇu rested. The annual festival coincides with the Tamil month of Āvaḻpōcchai (mid-Feb). Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple is in a rural setting near Tambaram.
