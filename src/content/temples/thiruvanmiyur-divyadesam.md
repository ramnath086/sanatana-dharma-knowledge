The Tiruvaramudreśvara Temple at Tiruvanmiyur (Chennai, Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Tiruvaramudreśvara (the Lord of the fragrant place). A 7th-century Pallava temple with beautiful early Chola bronzes. The annual Brahmotsavam and the Aṅga-pūrṇa festival are celebrated. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple tank (Pushkarinī) is associated with the legend of the sage Agastya. The nearby Parthasarathy Temple (Triplicane) is another Divya Deśam.
