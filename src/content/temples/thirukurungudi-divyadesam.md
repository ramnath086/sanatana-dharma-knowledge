The Nambi Temple at Tirukkurunkudi (Tirunelveli district, Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Nambi (the Lord) in a standing posture with Śrīdevi and Bhūdevi. At the foot of the Western Ghats (Mahendragiri Hills). The Cōḷa-period temple with a beautiful vimāna and the Tirupparkadal (milk ocean) tank. Glorified by Nammāḻvār (who was born nearby) and Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The annual Brahmotsavam and the Nammāḻvār-avatāra-utsavam are major festivals. One of the southernmost Divya Deśams.
