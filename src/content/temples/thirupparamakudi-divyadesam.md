The Tirupparāṅkuḍi Temple at Tirunelveli district (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Azhimukham (the face of the Lord). Located near the Courtallam waterfalls. The annual Brahmotsavam and the Theppam (float festival) are celebrated. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple tank (Pushkarinī) is associated with the Western Ghats.
