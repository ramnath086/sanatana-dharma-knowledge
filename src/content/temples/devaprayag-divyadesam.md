The Raghunāthajī Temple at Devaprayāga (Tehri Garhwal, Uttarakhand), a Divya Deśam at the confluence of the Alaknandā and Bhāgīrathī (forming the Gaṅgā). Dedicated to Viṣṇu as Raghunāthajī (Rāma). The legendary spot where Rāma performed penance after the Rāvaṇa war. The temple was rebuilt by the Garhwal kings. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The annual Rāma-navamī and the Gaṅgā-avataraṇa (Gaṅgā descent) festivals are celebrated. One of the five Prayāga confluences.
