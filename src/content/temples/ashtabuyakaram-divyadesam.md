The Aṣṭabhujākaram Temple (Eight-armed Viṣṇu) at Kāñcīpuram (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu with eight arms (aṣṭa-bhuja) holding the conch, discus, mace, lotus, bow, arrow, sword, and shield. The Cōḷa-period temple where Viṣṇu appeared to the sage Bhṛgu. Glorified by Poygai Āḻvār, Bhūta Āḻvār, Pēy Āḻvār, and Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The annual Brahmotsavam and the Bhṛgu-pūjā are celebrated. One of the cluster of Kāñcī Divya Deśams.
