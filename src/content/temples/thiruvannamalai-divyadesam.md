The Tiruvāṇṇamalai Temple at Tiruvāṇṇamalai (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Arunachalesvara (the Lord of the red hill). The temple is associated with the sacred hill (Arunachala) worshipped by Śaṅkara and Ramana Maharshi. The annual Brahmotsavam and the Mahashivaratri are celebrated. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple is also a major Śaiva pilgrimage site.
