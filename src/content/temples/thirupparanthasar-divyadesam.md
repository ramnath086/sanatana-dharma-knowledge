The Tirupparāntasar Temple at Tirunelveli district (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Parāntasar Perumāl. The annual festival is celebrated during the Tamil month of Aippāsi. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple is associated with the legend of the sage Parāntara and the divine intervention.
