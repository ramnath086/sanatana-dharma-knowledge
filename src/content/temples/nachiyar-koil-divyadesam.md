The Nācciyār Kōyil (Tirunāṟaiyūr) at Tiruppanandal near Kumbakonam (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Śrīnivāsa Perumāl with Goddess Nācciyār (Lakṣmī). The Cōḷa-period temple where Lakṣmī is enshrined as the primary deity (Nācciyār) — unique among Divya Deśams. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār and Nammāḻvār. The annual Garuḍa-sevā and the Tiruppāvai festival (Mārgaḻi) are celebrated. The temple architecture follows the madhyama (medium) type.
