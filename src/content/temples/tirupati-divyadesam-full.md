The Veṅkaṭeśvara Temple at Tirumala (Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh), a Divya Deśam and the most visited pilgrimage site in India (30-40 million annually). Dedicated to Viṣṇu as Veṅkaṭeśvara (Lord of Veṅkaṭa Hill). The Dravidian architecture with gold-plated vimāna (Ananda Nilayam). Glorified by Nammāḻvār, Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār, Kulasekhara Āḻvār, and Tiruppaṇ Āḻvār. The Tirumalā-Tirupati Devasthānams (TTD) manages the temple. The Brahmotsavam (9-day festival) and the weekly Śeṣa-vāhana-sevā are major events. The laddū-prasādam is world-famous.
