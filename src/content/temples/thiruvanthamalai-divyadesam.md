The Tiruvānthamālai Temple at Tirunelveli district (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Vānthamālai Perumāl (the Lord with garlands of flowers). The annual festival is celebrated during the Tamil month of Aippāsi. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple is associated with the legend of the flower garlands and the divine intervention.
