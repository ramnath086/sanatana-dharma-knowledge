The Ādi Jagannātha Temple at Tiruppullāṇi (near Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Ādi Jagannātha (the original Jagannātha) in a reclining posture. The legend: Rāma performed śrāddha for his father Daśaratha here; the temple marks the spot where Rāma prayed to Viṣṇu before crossing to Lanka. The sacred tīrtha (Darbha-śayana-tīrtha) is famous. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār and Nammāḻvār. The annual Rāma-navamī and the Ādi-amāvāsyā festivals are celebrated.
