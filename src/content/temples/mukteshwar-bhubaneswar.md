The Mukteśvara Temple at Bhubaneswar, Odisha, a 10th-century gem of Kalinga architecture, dedicated to Śiva as Mukteśvara (Lord of Liberation); its ornate torana (arched gateway), diamond-shaped latticed windows, and the Marichi Kunda tank are represented here as a catalogue entry.
