The Tiruppugalūr Temple at Tiruppugalūr (Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Tiruppugalūr Perumāl. Located near the historical battlefield of Kalinga-pattinam. The annual festival is celebrated during the Tamil month of Aṅgaḻirai. Glorified by Tirumazhisai Āḻvār and Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple has a beautiful gopuram and a large temple tank.
