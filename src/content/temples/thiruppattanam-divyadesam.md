The Tiruppaṭṭanam Temple near Thiruvananthapuram (Kerala/Tamil Nadu border), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Thiruppaṭṭanam Perumāl. Located in the ancient port town of Pāṇṭyāṅkal. The annual festival is celebrated during the Tamil month of Āvaḻpōcchai. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple is associated with the maritime trade of the ancient Tamil coast.
