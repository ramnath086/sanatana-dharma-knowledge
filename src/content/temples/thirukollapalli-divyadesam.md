The Tirukolla-paḍi Temple at Tirunelveli district (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Kollam-Paḍi Perumāl (the Lord of the collection/harvest). The annual festival is celebrated during the Tamil month of Aippāsi. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple is associated with the legend of the sage Agastya and the Kollam era.
