The Narasiṃha Temple at Jyotirmaṭha (Joshimath, Uttarakhand), a Divya Deśam and the winter seat of the Badrinath deity. Dedicated to Viṣṇu as Narasiṃha (the man-lion avatāra). One of the four Maṭhas established by Ādi Śaṅkara (the Uttara Maṭha). The deity is believed to be self-manifested (svayambhū). The temple is at 1,890m elevation. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The annual Narasiṃha-jayantī and the Badrinath opening/closing ceremonies are centered here. The nearby Kalpavṛkṣa and the cave of Ādi Śaṅkara are pilgrimage sites.
