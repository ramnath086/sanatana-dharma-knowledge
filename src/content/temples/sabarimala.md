The Ayyappa Temple at Sabarimala (ശബരിമല / சபரிமலை), Kerala, in the Periyar Tiger Reserve (Western Ghats) at 914m elevation. A major pilgrimage site (40-50 million annually, Nov-Jan) with a distinctive 41-day vrata (maṇḍala kālam / മണ്ഡല കാലം): black/blue clothes, brahmacarya, daily bath, no shaving, no footwear, vegetarian diet. The 18 holy steps (paṭinēṭṭāmpaṭi / பதினெட்டாம்படி) represent the 18 purāṇas/18 guṇas; only those carrying the irumuḍi (sacred bundle) may ascend. The deity is a yogic Śāstā in chin-mudrā. Makaravilakku (Makara Jyoti) on Makara Saṅkrānti (Jan 14/15) is the climax. The Vāvaru (Muslim devotee) shrine at Erumeli and the Peta (Petty) shrine en route exemplify communal harmony. The river Pampa is the sacred bathing ghāṭ. Managed by the Travancore Devaswom Board.
