The Tirupariśrīmala Temple at Tirupati district (Andhra Pradesh), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Pariśrīmala Perumāl (the fully auspicious Lord). The temple is known for its fine sculpture and architecture. The annual Parikrama and the Brahmotsavam are celebrated. Glorified by Tiruppaṇ Āḻvār. The temple tank (Pushkarinī) is associated with the story of the sage Agastya.
