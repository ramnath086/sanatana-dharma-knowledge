The Tiruvāṭi Temple at Tiruvāṭi (near Thiruvalla, Kerala), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Tiruvāṭi Perumāl. The temple is associated with the sage Durvāsa and the river Maṇimala. The annual festival coincides with the Makara-saṅkrāmaṇa. Glorified by Nammāḻvār. The Kerala-style architecture with a nalukettu layout. One of the Divya Deśams in the Travancore region.
