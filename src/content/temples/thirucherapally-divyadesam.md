The Tiruccherapalli Temple at Tiruchirappalli (Tamil Nadu), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Tiruccherapalli Perumāl. The annual festival is celebrated during the Tamil month of Aippāsi. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The temple is associated with the legend of the sacred place and the divine intervention.
