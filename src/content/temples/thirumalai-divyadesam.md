The Tirumalai Temple (Tirumalai Nambi) near Tirupati (Andhra Pradesh/Tamil Nadu border), a Divya Deśam dedicated to Viṣṇu as Nambi (the Lord). A hill temple at 600m elevation with 600 steps. The deity is in standing posture with Śrīdevi and Bhūdevi. Glorified by Tirumaṅgai Āḻvār. The annual Brahmotsavam and the Tiruppāvai festival are celebrated. One of the lesser-known but historically important Divya Deśams near the Tirupati complex.
