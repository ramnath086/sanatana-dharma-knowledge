Viṣu (വിഷു), the Malayalam New Year (Kolla Varṣam), observed on the first day of Meḍam (mid-April) when the Sun enters Meṣa Rāśi. The central ritual is Viṣukkaṇi (വിഷുക്കണി) — the auspicious first sight arranged the night before: a kaṇikonna (golden shower) flowers, rice, coconut, fruits, gold coins, mirror, and the Bhagavad Gītā or Rāmāyaṇa placed before Kṛṣṇa's image. Elders give Kaiṇeṭṭam (money gifts) to younger members. The Sādya feast includes viṣu-kaṭṭu (banana chips), māṅgā-caru (mango curry), and pāyaśam. Fireworks and the Viṣu-veḷḷa (boat race) at Aranmula. Marks the agricultural new year in Kerala.
