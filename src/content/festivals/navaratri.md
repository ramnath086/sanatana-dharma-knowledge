Navarātri (नवरात्रि / நவராத்திரி / നവരാത്രि / নৰরাত্রি / नवरात्री), the nine nights (nava-rātri) dedicated to the Goddess in her three guṇa-forms: Durgā (tamas, days 1-3), Lakṣmī (rajas, days 4-6), Sarasvatī (sattva, days 7-9). Autumn (Śarad, Sept-Oct) and Spring (Vasanta, Mar-Apr). Regional expressions: Durgā Pūjā (Bengal, pandals, immersion), Golu/Kolu (Tamil Nadu/Kerala, doll displays), Bathukamma (Telangana, flower stacks), Garbā/Dāṇḍiyā (Gujarat, circle dances), Mysore Dasara (royal procession), Ramlila (North, Rāma enactment), Ayudha Pūjā (tools/vehicles worship, day 9). Vijayadaśamī (day 10) = Dussehra (Rāma's victory) / Vidyarambha (initiation of learning). Fasting, devī-māhātmya recitation, kanya-pūjana (girls worshipped).
