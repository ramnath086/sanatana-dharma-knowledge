Dīpāvalī (Diwali), the festival of lights observed on the new moon of Kārttika (Oct–Nov); celebrating Rāma's return to Ayodhyā, Lakṣmī-pūjā, the victory of light over darkness; regional variations include Kālī Pūjā (Bengal), the worship of Yama (Dhanteras), and the Sikh Bandī Chhor Divas; its economic and cultural significance are represented here as a catalogue entry.
