The Tamil festival dedicated to Skanda/Murugaṇ on the full moon of Thai (Jan–Feb), observed with kavadi (burden-bearing), piercings, and processions; its regional prominence in Tamil Nadu, Malaysia, and Singapore is represented here as a catalogue entry.
