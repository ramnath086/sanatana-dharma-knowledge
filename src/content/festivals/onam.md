Oṇam (ഓണം), the state festival of Kerala, celebrated for ten days in the month of Ciṅgam (Aug-Sep). Commemorates the annual visit of Mahābali (Māvēli), the benevolent asura king, who was sent to Pātāla by Vāmana (Viṣṇu's dwarf avatāra) but granted a yearly return. Key elements: Pukkalam (floral rangoli), Oṇasadhya (feast on banana leaf with 20+ dishes), Vallam-kaḷi (snake boat races), Pulikkali (tiger dance), Tiruvaṭira (women's dance). The festival unites communities across religious lines. Glorified in the Nālāyira Divya Prabandham and associated with the Tṛkkākkāra Vāmana Temple.
