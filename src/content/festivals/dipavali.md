Dīpāvalī (दीपावली / தீபாவளி / ദീപാവലি / दिवाळी), the Festival of Lights (dīpa-āvalī = row of lamps). Five days: Dhanteras (wealth, Dhanvantarī), Narka Caturdashī (Kṛṣṇa slays Narakāsura), Amāvasyā (main day, Lakṣmī Pūjā, Rāma's return to Ayodhyā), Govardhan Pūjā (Kṛṣṇa lifts Govardhan), Bhāi Dooj (sibling bond). Lighting dīpas (oil lamps), rangoli, fireworks, sweets, new clothes. Regional: Kālī Pūjā (Bengal, same night), Bandi Chhor Divas (Sikh, Guru Hargobind's release), Bestu Varas (Gujarati New Year), Balipratipada (Maharashtra). Jain: Mahāvīra's nirvāṇa. The universal celebration of light over darkness, knowledge over ignorance.
