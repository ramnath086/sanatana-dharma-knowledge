Guru Pūrṇimā, the full moon day of Āṣāḍha (Jun–Jul), dedicated to the guru (spiritual teacher); traditionally associated with Vyāsa (the compiler of the Vedas), observed by disciples offering pūjā, dakṣiṇā, and seeking blessings; its observance in Ādi Śaṅkara maṭhas, Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava, and other traditions is represented here as a catalogue entry.
