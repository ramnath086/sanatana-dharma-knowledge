Rāma Navamī, the festival of Rāma's birth, observed on the ninth day (navamī) of the bright fortnight of Caitra (Mar–Apr); Rāma-pūjā, Rāmāyaṇa recitation, Rāmalīlā enactments, and the ceremonial wedding (Sītā-Rāma kalyāṇam) in South India; its observance in Ayodhyā, Rāmeśvaram, and Bhadrachalam are represented here as a catalogue entry.
