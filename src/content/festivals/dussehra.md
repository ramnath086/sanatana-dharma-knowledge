Vijayadaśamī (Dussehra), the tenth day after Navarātri, celebrating the victory of good over evil: Durgā's victory over Mahiṣāsura (East/West India) and Rāma's victory over Rāvaṇa (North India); observed with Rāmalīlā enactments, effigy burning (Rāvaṇa, Kumbhakarṇa, Meghanāda), and the worship of weapons (śastra-pūjā); its regional variations are represented here as a catalogue entry.
