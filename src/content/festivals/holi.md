Holī, the spring festival of colours observed on the full moon of Phālguna (Feb–Mar); the evening Holikā Dahan (bonfire) commemorating Prahlāda's survival, and the day of colour play (Raṅgavalī Holī) celebrating divine love (Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa); regional variations include Lathmār Holī (Barsana) and Hola Mohalla (Sikh, Punjab) are represented here as a catalogue entry.
