Meenakari (enamelling), the art of decorating metal surfaces (gold, silver, copper) with colored enamel; its Persian origins, centers in Jaipur, Udaipur, Delhi, and Varanasi; its application on jewelry, weapons, and decorative objects; the champlevé and cloisonné techniques are represented here as a catalogue entry.
