The Warli painting tradition of the Warli tribe (Maharashtra–Gujarat border), characterized by white geometric figures on mud walls depicting daily life, nature, and rituals; its ritual origins (marriage, harvest), the use of rice paste, and its contemporary adaptation to paper/canvas are represented here as a catalogue entry.
