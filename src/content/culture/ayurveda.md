Āyurveda (आयुर्वेद) is the traditional Indian science of life ('life-knowledge'), codified in the Suśruta-saṃhitā (surgery, the 'sharp' bheṣaja) and the Caraka-saṃhitā (medicine), with Vāgbhaṭa's Aṣṭāṅga-saṅgraha. Its foundation is the tridoṣa theory (vāta, pitta, kapha): health (svasthya) is balance of the doṣas with digestive fire (agni) and the seven dhātu; the 'five' great elements (mahābhūta) underlie form. It prescribes āhāra (diet), vihāra (lifestyle) and seasonal (ṛtu) treatments, and classifies the three constitutional types (doṣa-prakṛti).
