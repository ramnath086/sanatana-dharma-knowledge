The Kalamkari (pen-craft) painting tradition of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana (Srikalahasti and Machilipatnam styles); hand-painted or block-printed cotton textiles with natural dyes depicting Rāmāyaṇa, Mahābhārata, and Purāṇic themes; its Persian influence and contemporary revival are represented here as a catalogue entry.
