Bidriware, the metal handicraft of Bidar (Karnataka), characterized by silver inlay on a blackened zinc-copper alloy; its Persian origins (14th century), motifs (floral, geometric, calligraphic), and products (vases, boxes, jewelry); its GI tag and contemporary revival are represented here as a catalogue entry.
