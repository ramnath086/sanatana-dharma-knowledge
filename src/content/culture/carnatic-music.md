Karnāṭaka Saṅgīta (कर्नाटक संगीत / கர்நாடக சங்கீதம் / കർണാടക സംഗീതం), the classical music tradition of South India (Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana). Core: rāga (melodic framework), tāḷa (rhythmic cycle), and compositional forms — varṇam, kṛti, tillāṇa, javali, padam, svarajati. The Trinity (18th-19th c.): Tyāgarāja (kṛti perfection, bhakti), Muttusvāmi Dīkṣitar (Sanskrit, śrī vidyā, western notes), Śyāma Śāstri (Svarajati, Kāmākṣi). Pedagogy: sarali varisai, jaṇṭai varisai, daṭṭu varisai, alankāram, gītaṃ, svarajati, varṇam, kṛti. The 72 melakarta rāga system (Venkatamakhin). Instruments: vīṇā, veṇu, violin, mṛdaṅgam, ghaṭam, kanjira, morsing. The December Music Season (Chennai) is the world's largest classical music festival.

The classical music tradition of South India, with its rāga, tāḷa, and compositional forms (kṛti, varṇam, tillāṇa).
