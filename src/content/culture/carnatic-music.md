The classical music tradition of South India, with its rāga, tāḷa, and compositional forms (kṛti, varṇam, tillāṇa); its history, composers (Tyāgarāja, Muttusvāmi Dīkṣitar, Śyāma Śāstri), and pedagogy are represented here as a catalogue entry.
