The Pahari miniature painting schools of the Himalayan foothills: Basohli (bold colors, geometric), Kangra (lyrical, naturalistic), Guler, Chamba, Mandi, Kullu, and Jammu; their themes (Gīta Govinda, Bhāgavata Purāṇa, Nala-Damayantī, portraits), the delicate lines, soft colors, and their refinement of the Mughal style are represented here as a catalogue entry.
