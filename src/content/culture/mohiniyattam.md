Mohiniyāṭṭam (മോഹിനിയാട്ടം), Kerala's classical solo dance (lasya style), traditionally performed by women. Reconstructed in the 1930s by V.N. Menon and Kala Mandalam (Vallathol Narayana Menon, Mukundraj). Key elements: graceful circular movements (cuzhippu), swaying gait (gamana), subtle facial expressions (sāttvika abhinaya), white/cream costume with gold border (kasavu), jasmine hair adornment. The repertoire: colkeṭṭu (invocation), jati-svaram, varṇam, padam, tillānā. Music: Sopana style, Carnatic rāgas (Tōḍi, Bhairavi, Kalyāṇi), accompaniment: veena, veṇu, maddaḷam, eḷattāḷam. Themes: Śṛṅgāra (love), Bhakti (devotion to Viṣṇu/Kṛṣṇa). Mohinī = Viṣṇu's enchantress form (distributing amṛta). Recognized by Sangeet Natak Akademi.
