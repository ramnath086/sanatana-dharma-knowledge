Dhanurveda (धनुर्वेद) is the 'bow-Veda,' an Upaveda of the Yajur Veda treating archery and arms as the martial lore of the kṣatriyas. It covers the bow and arrow (dhana-bāṇa), the sword (khadga), the spear (śakti), and the theory of the bow's constituents (wood, fiber, sinew). It is cited in the Mahābhārata's war passages as the astra-śāstra (weapon-science) and underlies the later gadā-yuddha (mace-fighting) and mallayuddha (wrestling) traditions.
