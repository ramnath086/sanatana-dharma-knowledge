Kāṭhakaḷi (കഥകളി), Kerala's classical dance-drama (17th century synthesis of Kṛṣṇanāṭṭam, Rāmanāṭṭam, and folk traditions). Key elements: elaborate vesham (makeup/costume) — paccā (green, noble), kaṭṭi (knife, anti-hero), kari (black, demoness), taṭi (beard: white=divine, red=evil, black=hunter), minukku (radiant, female/sage); the navarasas (nine emotions) through facial muscles (netrābhinaya); the āṭṭakathās (scripts) from Mahābhārata, Rāmāyaṇa, Bhāgavata (e.g., Nalacaritam, Kalyāṇasaugandhikam); sopāna-saṅgīta (Sopana music) with ceṇḍa, maṭalam, elathāḷam; rigorous gurukula training (8-10 years) at Kala Mandalam, Kottakkal. Recognized by UNESCO as Intangible Cultural Heritage. A synthesis of nṛtya (dance), nṛtta (pure movement), and nāṭya (drama).

The classical dance-drama tradition of Kerala, with elaborate costume, makeup, and facial expressions.
