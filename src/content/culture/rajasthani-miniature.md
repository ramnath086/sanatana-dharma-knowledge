The Rajasthani miniature painting schools: Mewar (Udaipur), Marwar (Jodhpur), Bundi, Kota, Kishangarh, Jaipur, and Bikaner; their themes (Rāgamālā, Rasikapriyā, Bhāgavata Purāṇa, Rāmāyaṇa, court portraits), the use of natural pigments, the bold lines and vibrant colors, and their Mughal influence are represented here as a catalogue entry.
