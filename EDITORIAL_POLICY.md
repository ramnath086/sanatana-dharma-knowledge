# Editorial Policy

Sanatana Dharma Knowledge Portal is a public, educational reference project. It aims to be useful without flattening a diverse and living set of traditions into a single voice.

## Principles

- Prefer original summaries, transparent citations and links to public sources.
- Distinguish established reference material, interpretation, tradition-specific claims and project placeholders.
- Do not fabricate scripture, Sanskrit, quotations, dates, lineages or historical claims.
- Represent disagreements and variation where they matter. Do not present one school or community as the whole tradition.
- Use diacritics and transliteration consistently, while keeping pages welcoming to beginners.
- Treat living communities, sacred places and practices with respect. Avoid exoticising language.
- Mark unfinished records as `sample` or `draft` until they have been reviewed.

## Review

New content should identify its sources, note the perspective or tradition it represents, and be reviewed for accuracy, clarity and respectful framing before publication.
