# Claims and Conflicts

Claims are structured statements attached to an entity. Claim types include definition, textual, historical, traditional, linguistic, ritual, philosophical, geographical, biographical and bibliographical.

Conflicting claims are preserved rather than silently synthesized. A conflict can be marked as conflict, variant, different-tradition, different-edition or uncertain, with separate evidence and editorial notes.

Traditional accounts, historical/scholarly accounts and modern interpretations should remain distinguishable when they differ materially.
