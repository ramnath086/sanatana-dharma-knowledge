# Rights and Licensing

A resource being visible online does not imply permission to copy, download, redistribute or commercially use it.

Supported rights states include public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC BY-NC, CC BY-NC-SA, permission required, link-only, all rights reserved and unknown. Every unknown, all-rights-reserved or permission-required source defaults to link-only behavior.

The portal may catalog a resource with title, organization, metadata, short original description and official link without hosting its contents. Rights notes and license URLs should be recorded when verified. The compiler blocks published entities whose sources have unknown rights.
