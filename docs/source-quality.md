# Source Quality Rules

Source selection considers scope, provenance, authority context, language, rights and fit for the specific claim. Authority categories are descriptive, not a universal ranking.

Traditional sources are handled with attention to living practice and lineage. Academic sources are handled with attention to edition, method and scholarly scope. Government and institutional archives may be strongest for collection metadata. Disagreements are represented as separate claims or perspectives, not erased by a single score.

Citations should identify the source by name, organization and official link. Body text should not expose raw URLs or imply permission to reproduce third-party material.
