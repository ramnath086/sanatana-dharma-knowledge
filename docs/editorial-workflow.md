# Editorial Workflow

The editorial stages are:

`DISCOVERED → RESEARCHING → DRAFT → SOURCE-CHECK → EDITORIAL-REVIEW → TRADITIONAL-REVIEW → APPROVED → PUBLISHED`

Not every record needs every stage. Traditional review is used when a living practice or sampradaya-specific account requires it. Scholarly review and traditional review are distinct. Neither is a universal ranking.

`/editorial/sources` and `/editorial/review` are internal routes and are intentionally absent from public navigation. They show drafts and review signals, but no internal route should be treated as a publication API.
