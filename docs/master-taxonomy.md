# Master Taxonomy

The master taxonomy is a controlled vocabulary of domains, not a universal parent-child hierarchy. An entity may belong to several domains and should also use explicit relationships when a hierarchy would oversimplify a tradition.

Domains include Śruti, Vedāṅga, Smṛti, Itihāsa, Purāṇa, Dharmaśāstra, Darśana, Vedānta, Āgama, Tantra, Yoga, Practice, Ritual, Mantra, Stotra, Deity, Rishi, Ācārya, Teacher, Sampradāya, Tradition, Temple, Pilgrimage, Festival, Sanskrit, Manuscript, Inscription, Commentary, Translation, Book, Audio, Video, Art, Music, Culture, Regional Tradition, Concept, Glossary and Scholarship.

Frontmatter classifications are validated against this vocabulary. Relationships such as `belongs-to-domain`, `subtype-of`, `part-of`, `associated-with` and `practiced-in` carry meaning that should not be inferred from category labels alone.
