# Knowledge Expansion

Phase 8 expands the corpus category-by-category rather than through bulk generation. The current expansion covers Vedāṅga, core concepts, practices, festivals, sampradāya categories and domain nodes.

New records begin as Markdown candidates. They are checked for duplicate IDs, taxonomy membership, source references, rights, relationships and editorial readiness. Draft and review records remain in the editorial registry and do not enter public search or sitemap output.
