# Foundational Content

Phase 7 adds a curated seed of 50 entity records. Forty-eight are published because they have concise original descriptions, stable metadata, verified editorial state and link-only source rights; two remain draft editorial records.

The seed covers Vedic collections, Upaniṣadic categories, Itihāsa, Darśana, Vedānta schools, concepts, practices, people, deities, festivals and taxonomy nodes. Thirty records form the first public release; the remaining records stay in the editorial registry for later review. It deliberately avoids quotations, invented dates, copied translations and claims that require unsupported historical certainty.
