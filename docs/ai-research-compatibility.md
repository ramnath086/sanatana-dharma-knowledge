# Research Pipeline Compatibility

The model is designed for a future assisted research workflow:

1. Discover a candidate source.
2. Register source metadata and copyright terms.
3. Extract candidate information.
4. Create a draft entity.
5. Suggest relationships and citations.
6. Detect conflicts and flag uncertainty.
7. Submit for editorial or tradition-specific review.
8. Publish only after verification and copyright checks.

No autonomous web scraping or autonomous publication is implemented. Human review remains the publication boundary.
