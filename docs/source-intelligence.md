# Source Intelligence

Sources are discovery and provenance records, not automatic authority rankings. A traditional institution may be the appropriate source for a living ritual account; an academic edition may be appropriate for textual history; a government archive may be appropriate for metadata.

Canonical source records live under `src/content/sources/`. Each record identifies its organization, scope, language, source type, authority context, rights state, monitoring state and intended use in the portal. Unknown rights are recorded as unknown and default to link-only behavior.

The initial seed set includes Vedic Heritage Portal, GRETIL, SARIT, The Sanskrit Library, Muktabodha Digital Library and Institut Français de Pondichéry digital collections. These are source records only; the portal does not copy their content.
